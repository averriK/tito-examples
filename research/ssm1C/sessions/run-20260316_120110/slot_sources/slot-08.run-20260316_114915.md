## SLOT 8: Local Sources and Site Interrogation

The Rampura Agucha site ($74.7413^\circ$E, $25.8474^\circ$N) is located in Rajasthan, peninsular India. All seismic sources surrounding and including the site in the areal source model belong to the Stable Continental Crust (SCC) tectonic region type, characteristic of intraplate continental settings with relatively low seismicity rates, moderate maximum magnitudes, and predominantly shallow-to-intermediate seismogenic depths. No subduction, active shallow crustal, or fault sources are present in the immediate vicinity of the site. [KB:site_sources.md][KB:site_sources_data.md]^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The SCC tectonic regime attribution and site coordinates are explicitly stated in site_sources.md. The classification of both containing zones (z919 and z932) as SCC is confirmed in both site_sources.md and site_sources_data.md. The absence of subduction or fault sources at the site is consistent with the SCC assignment and the statement in site_sources.md that "No fault sources are included in the IND source model."]

The site is contained within two overlapping areal seismogenic zones, both present in the areal source model branch (weight 0.40). Zone z919 (Peninsular India Layer 1) represents the shallow crustal seismogenic layer: upper seismogenic depth 0.0 km, lower depth 25.0 km, mean hypocentral depth 15.0 km, $M_{\max}$ = 7.20 Mw (base; epistemic range 6.90-7.50 Mw), $a$-value 2.73, $b$-value 0.72 (base), and reverse-faulting focal mechanism (strike $290^\circ$, dip $61^\circ$, rake $123^\circ$). Zone z932 (Peninsular India Layer 2) represents the intermediate seismogenic layer: upper depth 25.0 km, lower depth 70.0 km, mean hypocentral depth 25.0 km, $M_{\max}$ = 6.50 Mw (base; epistemic range 6.20-6.80 Mw), $a$-value 4.15, $b$-value 0.93 (base), and sinistral strike-slip focal mechanism (strike $239^\circ$, dip $67^\circ$, rake $8^\circ$). [KB:site_sources.md][KB:site_sources_data.md]^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: All parameter values for z919 and z932 are explicitly tabulated in site_sources.md and confirmed in site_sources_data.md. The Mmax epistemic ranges (z919: 6.90-7.50 Mw; z932: 6.20-6.80 Mw) correspond to the base values plus the maxMagGRRelative perturbation of +/-0.30 Mw, which is arithmetically consistent with the logic tree specification.]

Based on the characteristics of the enclosing SCC zones, the seismic events most relevant to ground-motion hazard at the Rampura Agucha site are shallow- to intermediate-depth intraplate crustal earthquakes. For zone z919, the credible magnitude range extends up to $M_{\max}$ = 7.20 Mw (base) at depths in the range 10-25 km; for zone z932, the credible range extends to $M_{\max}$ = 6.50 Mw (base) at depths of 25-70 km. The SCC regime is consistent with a low-to-moderate background seismicity rate but a finite probability of moderate to large intraplate events, as demonstrated historically by the 2001 Bhuj earthquake ($M$ 7.7) in Gujarat, India [-@GEM_IND]. Definitive identification of the controlling source requires deaggregation of the PSHA results; the sources identified here represent geometric candidates from source-to-site distance analysis.
