## SLOT 3: Iron versus aluminum comparison

Iron and aluminum differ substantially across all three physical properties documented in the KB. Iron has a melting point of 1538 C versus aluminum's 660 C, a difference of 878 C, indicating that iron maintains its solid state at temperatures well above aluminum's melting threshold. Iron's density of 7.874 g/cm$^{3}$ is approximately 2.9 times aluminum's 2.70 g/cm$^{3}$, making iron considerably heavier per unit volume. Iron also carries a higher atomic number (26) compared to aluminum (13), placing it further along the periodic table. Taken together, iron is denser, thermally more stable, and heavier than aluminum, while aluminum is substantially lighter and melts at a much lower temperature.
