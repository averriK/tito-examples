## SLOT 3: Iron versus aluminum comparison

Iron and aluminum differ substantially in both properties reported in the knowledge base. Iron has a melting point of $1538\,^\circ\text{C}$, compared with $660\,^\circ\text{C}$ for aluminum, a difference of $878\,^\circ\text{C}$ that reflects iron's considerably greater thermal stability and resistance to softening or melting at elevated temperatures. In terms of density, iron at $7.874\,\text{g/cm}^3$ is approximately 2.9 times denser than aluminum at $2.70\,\text{g/cm}^3$, indicating that aluminum offers a substantial mass advantage for applications in which minimizing weight is a priority.
