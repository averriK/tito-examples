## SLOT 3: Iron versus aluminum comparison

The principal property differences between iron and aluminum, as documented in the knowledge base, are summarized below. [KB:metals.md]
- **Melting point**: Iron melts at $1538\ ^\circ\text{C}$; aluminum melts at $660\ ^\circ\text{C}$, a difference of $878\ ^\circ\text{C}$. Iron exhibits substantially greater thermal resistance under high-temperature conditions. [KB:metals.md]
- **Density**: Iron has a density of $7.874\ \text{g/cm}^3$; aluminum has a density of $2.70\ \text{g/cm}^3$. Aluminum is approximately 2.9 times less dense, resulting in a substantially lower mass per unit volume. [KB:metals.md]
- **Atomic number**: Iron has atomic number 26; aluminum has atomic number 13. This difference reflects their respective positions in the periodic table and their distinct electron shell configurations. [KB:metals.md]
