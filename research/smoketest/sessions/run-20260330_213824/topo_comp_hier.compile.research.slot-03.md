## SLOT 3: Iron versus aluminum comparison

The principal property differences between iron and aluminum, as documented in the knowledge base, are summarized below. [@MetalsKB]

- **Melting point**: Iron melts at $1538\,^\circ\text{C}$; aluminum melts at $660\,^\circ\text{C}$, a difference of $878\,^\circ\text{C}$. Iron exhibits substantially greater thermal resistance under high-temperature conditions. [@MetalsKB][@RSCIron][@RSCAluminium]
- **Density**: Iron has a density of $7.874\,\text{g/cm}^3$; aluminum has a density of $2.70\,\text{g/cm}^3$. Aluminum is approximately 2.9 times less dense, resulting in a substantially lower mass per unit volume. [@MetalsKB][@RSCIron][@RSCAluminium]
- **Atomic number**: Iron has atomic number 26; aluminum has atomic number 13, reflecting their respective positions in the periodic table. [@MetalsKB][@RSCIron][@RSCAluminium]
