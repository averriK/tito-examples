
The fundamental period of the embankment can be expressed in terms of the shear-wave velocity at the base $v_S^{o}$, which can be estimated from the shear stiffness $G_o$ at the base $z=H_s$ and the density $\rho$ of the material using the relation $v_S^{o}=\sqrt{G_o/\rho}$. The expression [-@eq-TS] provides an estimate of different fundamental periods $T_s^{(j)}$ where, $a^{(j)}=f(m_o,\lambda_o)$ is the $j$th-root of the characteristic equation (eigenvalues) that depend on the homogeneity ratio $m_o$ and the truncation ratio $\lambda_o$.

$$T_s^{(j)}\simeq\dfrac{4\,\pi\,H_{max}}{a^{(j)}\,(2-m_o)\,v_S^{o}}$$ {#eq-TS}

The truncation ratio can be defined in terms of the height $H_{max}$, the berm width $b_o$ and the average slope $tan\beta=1/s$ in the form of [@eq-LAMBDA], where $B_{max}$ is the base width of the embankment. The truncation ratio $\lambda_o$ is a measure of the influence of the berm width $B_o$ on the fundamental period of the embankment. 

$$\lambda_o=\dfrac{B_o}{B_{max}}=\dfrac{B_o}{2 \ H_{max} \ s + B_o }$$ {#eq-LAMBDA}

For a given height $H_{max}$, the fundamental period of the embankment increases with the truncation ratio $\lambda_o$. The homogeneity ratio $m_o$ reflects the law of variation of the shear modulus $G(z)\approx G_o (z/H_{max})^{m_o}$, where $G_o$ is the (maximum) shear moduli at the base $(z=0)$. The shear moduli $G(z)$ of an arbitrary layer at depth $z$ can be estimated in terms of the void ratio $e_o$ and the octahedral stresses $\sigma_o(z)$ from [@eq-SM], where $p_{ref}$ is a reference pressure, $A$, $C_e$, and $n$ are material-dependent constants and $e_0$ is the void ratio of the material, and $\sigma_o(z)$ is the octahedral stress at depth $z$ [@Ishihara1997].  

$$G(z,e_o)\approx A \frac{(C_e-1)^2}{1+e_o} \left(\frac{\sigma'_o(z)}{p_{ref}} \right)^n$$ {#eq-SM}
