## SLOT 5: Modern Methods Post-Bray (2019) and Fundamental Period Estimation

Estimation of the maximum base stiffness constitutes a prerequisite for the calculation of the fundamental period of a slope. The relevant stiffness parameter is the small-strain shear modulus $G_0$ - equivalently expressed through the shear wave velocity $v_S^o = \sqrt{G_0/\rho}$, where $\rho$ is the material density - evaluated at the base of the slope at depth $z = H_{max}$. This maximum-stiffness condition corresponds to the small-strain regime in which the soil or tailings material behaves approximately elastically, before any nonlinear stiffness degradation occurs. [KB:periods.md]^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The relationship between $G_0$, $v_S^o$, and density is a fundamental result of elastic wave theory directly stated in the KB (periods.md). The evaluation at the base depth and the association with the small-strain elastic regime are consistent with the KB description and standard soil dynamics practice.]

The shear modulus $G$ at an arbitrary depth $z$ is estimated as a function of the void ratio $e_o$ and the octahedral effective stress $\sigma'_o(z)$, following the formulation of Ishihara [@Ishihara1997][KB:periods.md]. In this expression, $A$ is a material stiffness constant, $C_e$ governs the sensitivity of stiffness to void ratio, $n$ is a stress-dependency exponent, $p_{ref}$ is a reference pressure (conventionally atmospheric pressure), and $e_0$ is the void ratio. The model parameters are determined from laboratory tests on undisturbed samples of the material, and published tables in Ishihara (1997) report calibrated parameter sets for a range of soil types classified by USCS designation, together with the ranges of void ratio over which the calibrations are valid. [KB:periods.md]^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The Ishihara (1997) shear modulus model and its functional dependence on void ratio and effective octahedral stress are directly stated in the KB (periods.md). The parameter identification ($A$, $C_e$, $n$, $p_{ref}$) and the reference to Ishihara's parameter tables are faithfully reproduced from the source.]

$$G(z,\,e_o) \approx A\,\frac{(C_e-1)^2}{1+e_o}\,\left(\frac{\sigma'_o(z)}{p_{ref}}\right)^n$$

The shear stiffness profile follows a power-law variation with depth, expressible as $G(z) \approx G_o (z/H_{max})^{m_o}$, where $G_o$ is the maximum shear modulus at the base $z = H_{max}$ and $m_o$ is the homogeneity ratio governing the shape of the stiffness profile. A value of $m_o = 0$ corresponds to a depth-invariant (homogeneous) profile, while positive values of $m_o$ indicate stiffness increasing with depth. The shear wave velocity at the base, $v_S^o = \sqrt{G_o/\rho}$, and the homogeneity ratio $m_o$ together constitute the two stiffness-related inputs required for fundamental period estimation. In practice, the Ishihara model is evaluated at multiple depths to construct a full $G(z)$ profile, which is then fitted to the power-law form to extract $G_o$ and $m_o$ for the relevant slope geometry and material. The practical fitting procedure follows logically from the model structure.]
