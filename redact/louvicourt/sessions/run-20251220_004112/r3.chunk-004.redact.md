### 1.1 Scope of Work

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Structure preserved. No privacy issues in the heading alone.]

The purpose of this SSSHA is to quantify earthquake ground motions with an annual exceedance probability (AEP) of 1/2,475, 1/5,000, and 1/10,000. These AEPs are approximately equivalent to return periods of 2,475-, 5,000-, and 1/10,000-years, respectively.

- Review of seismic source models developed by the Geological Survey of Canada (GSC) and Natural Resources Canada (NRCan) for the 2020 National Building Code of Canada (2020 NBCC).
