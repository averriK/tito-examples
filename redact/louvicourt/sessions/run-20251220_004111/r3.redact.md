### 1.1 Scope of Work

The purpose of this SSSHA is to quantify earthquake ground motions with an annual exceedance probability (AEP) of 1/2,475, 1/5,000, and 1/10,000. These AEPs are approximately equivalent to return periods of 2,475-, 5,000-, and 1/10,000-years, respectively.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: No identifying information present; preserved all technical content including AEP values and return periods; paragraph structure intact]

XXXXXXX undertook the following major tasks for the SSSHA:^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Redacted company name; preserved technical context and list introduction]

- Acquisition and review of available information on historical earthquakes, regional tectonics, and potential faults within about 600 km of the XXXXXXX site.
- Review of seismic source models developed by the Geological Survey of Canada (GSC) and Natural Resources Canada (NRCan) for the 2020 National Building Code of Canada (2020 NBCC).
- Development of logic trees to capture epistemic uncertainty in the site-specific seismic source model inputs and ground motion models (GMMs).
- Completion of site-specific probabilistic seismic hazard analyses (PSHA) to produce mean and fractile hazard curves for selected spectral periods (peak ground acceleration [PGA], 0.2 seconds [s], 0.5 s, 1.0 s, and 2.0 s), and uniform hazard spectra (UHS) for selected AEPs (1/2,475, 1/5,000, and 1/10,000).
- Magnitude-distance-epsilon deaggregation of site-specific seismic hazard to identify the earthquake magnitudes and distances that make the greater contribution to the site hazard for the 1/10,000 AEP at PGA, 0.2 s, 0.5 s, and 1.0 s.
- Development of eight, two-horizontal-component earthquake acceleration-time histories (EATHs), matched to the 1/10,000 AEP uniform hazard spectral accelerations to support dynamic stability, settlement, and liquefaction analyses at the site, as needed by the project engineers.
