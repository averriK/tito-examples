# Heap leaching – pregnant solution terminology demo

This is a minimal source document to exercise the new `translate` workflow with TBX termbases.

In heap leaching operations, **pregnant solution** refers to the process solution that has percolated through the ore heap and now contains dissolved metal in economically relevant concentrations. The pregnant solution is collected in a lined collection sump at the toe of the heap leach pad and then pumped to the solvent‑extraction and electrowinning (SX–EW) plant.

For this demo, you MUST treat “pregnant solution” as a single technical term, not as an everyday biological metaphor. The correct Spanish technical term in hydrometallurgy is **“solución rica”**, whereas literal calques such as “solución embarazada” are incorrect and should be treated as forbidden in professional reports.
