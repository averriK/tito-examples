### Estabilidad superficial de coberturas

Se presentan los factores de seguridad minimos, probabilidad de falla y la capa en la que se produce la falla. Debido a la variabilidad de los parametros de suelo considerados en el analisis de sensibilidad, se presentan los valores medios y el rango de los resultados de los FoS determinados.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The presentation of minimum, mean, and maximum factors of safety along with probability of failure and identification of the critical layer is consistent with best practice for probabilistic slope stability analysis. Reporting ranges of FoS values from sensitivity analysis provides useful information about parameter influence on results [WEB:https://cdnsciencepub.com/doi/10.1139/t10-044]. However, the interpretation of these results must account for the significant uncertainties in input parameters noted earlier, including lack of independent validation and reliance on literature values rather than site-specific testing.]

Como se puede apreciar, algunos valores de FoS no alcanzan los valores minimos requeridos indicados anteriormente, pero todas las probabilidades de falla asociadas se encuentran por debajo del 10%.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The observation that some mean FoS values fall below the minimum required values but all associated probabilities of failure remain below 10% highlights the difference between deterministic and probabilistic acceptance criteria. This outcome is consistent with the earlier statement that 10% maximum PoF corresponds to FoS > 1.3 for low consequence and low data confidence situations. However, cases where mean FoS < 1.3 but PoF < 10% represent marginal conditions that warrant careful consideration, particularly given the acknowledged low confidence in material parameters. The use of PoF < 10% as the governing acceptance criterion for cases failing deterministic FoS criteria depends on validation of the consequence classification and acceptance of the probabilistic threshold from the cited but unverified Hawley2017 reference.]

Del analisis realizado, se observa que en dos casos los factores de seguridad obtenidos no cumplen con el valor minimo recomendado de 1.3 para los valores medios. Especificamente, estas situaciones corresponden al uso de suelo organico sobre pendientes de 1.7:1 y suelos finos sobre pendientes de 2.2:1. Todos los factores de seguridad obtenidos resultan mayores a la unidad para todo el rango de parametros materiales considerados.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The explicit acknowledgment that two cases fail to meet the 1.30 minimum FoS criterion (organic soil on 1.7:1 slopes and fine soils on 2.2:1 slopes) is important. However, the characterization appears to undercount the deficient cases: the table shows Cover A on 1.7:1 slopes with mean FoS = 1.17 (appears in Sections 1, 3, 4, 5, 6), Cover B on 2.2:1 slopes with mean FoS = 1.18 (Section 3), and arguably Cover I on 2.2:1 slopes is also borderline at mean FoS = 1.32. The statement that all FoS values remain above unity (1.0) across the full parameter range is positive, indicating no predicted failure even with pessimistic parameter combinations. However, minimum FoS values as low as 1.06-1.08 in the sensitivity analysis leave very little margin of safety and would likely be unacceptable under more stringent consequence classifications. For test panels, these marginal results may be acceptable with close monitoring, but would typically require design modifications for final closure implementation.]

En cuanto a la evaluacion probabilistica de la estabilidad, en la mayoria de las configuraciones los resultados obtenidos muestran valores de PoF menores al 1%, lo que representa una muy baja probabilidad de falla bajo la variabilidad considerada para los parametros resistentes. Las unicas excepciones se registraron en dos situaciones puntuales:

- Para el suelo organico (topsoil) en la capa superficial de taludes con pendiente 1.7:1 (Secciones 1, 3, 4, 5 y 6 - Cobertura tipo A),
- Y para la arcilla utilizada en taludes de pendiente 2.2:1 (Seccion 3 - Cobertura tipo B).

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The probabilistic results showing most configurations with PoF < 1% and only two configurations with elevated PoF (topsoil on 1.7:1 slopes at 8.1% and clay on 2.2:1 slopes at 5.13%) are consistent with the FoS results in the table. The low PoF values for most configurations reflect the probabilistic analysis accounting for parameter variability using the assumed distributions (10% CoV for friction angle, 25% CoV for cohesion). However, these PoF estimates are only as reliable as the assumed parameter distributions, which are based on literature values rather than site-specific statistical data given the rejected laboratory test results and lack of independent validation. The actual probability of failure could be higher if the true parameter variability exceeds the assumed distributions or if systematic bias exists in the mean values.]

Incluso en estos casos, los valores de PoF se mantuvieron en niveles aceptablemente bajos, con probabilidades de falla entre el 5% y el 10%.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The characterization of PoF values between 5% and 10% as "acceptably low" relies on the acceptance criterion of maximum 10% PoF stated earlier based on the Hawley2017 reference for low consequence, low data confidence situations. While the values are below this threshold, PoF in the 5-10% range represents relatively high probability compared to typical design practice for permanent structures. For comparison, a reliability index of 3 (corresponding to approximately 0.1% failure probability) is commonly cited as a minimum for stable slopes [WEB:https://researchrepository.wvu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1993&context=etd]. The acceptance of 5-10% PoF is appropriate only if the consequence classification as "low" is valid and if stakeholders agree with this risk tolerance. For test panels subject to monitoring and potential remediation, this may be acceptable, but would typically be too high for final closure design without additional mitigation measures.]

### Estabilidad global de taludes

Como aproximacion, la verificacion de estabilidad global del talud se verifico para la pendiente maxima de 1.7:1 y una altura de 50 m considerando un unico material de relleno (waste rock). Mediante los metodos de equilibrio limite de Morgenstern-Price y Spencer se analizaron fallas circulares y poligonales y se determino un coeficiente de seguridad minimo de 1.60, el cual satisface los valores indicados previamente.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The global stability analysis yielding a minimum factor of safety of 1.60 comfortably exceeds the specified criterion of 1.50 for static global slope stability. The use of Morgenstern-Price and Spencer methods for analyzing both circular and polygonal failure surfaces is appropriate and represents rigorous limit equilibrium analysis [WEB:https://www.rocscience.com/help/slide2/documentation/slide-model/project-settings/methods/analysis-methods]. However, several aspects limit confidence in this result: (1) The analysis is described as an "approximation" using simplified geometry (single 50-meter section at 1.7:1 slope), (2) The model assumes homogeneous waste rock without accounting for cover layers or potential weak zones, (3) The critical foundation assumption (competent bedrock preventing deep failures) cannot be independently verified, (4) No seismic analysis results are presented despite seismic evaluation being within the stated scope, and (5) The waste rock strength parameters rely on a site-specific nonlinear function from Piteau2022 that cannot be verified. The FoS = 1.60 result is plausible for a well-drained waste rock dump on competent foundation, but the simplified analysis approach means actual global stability under all conditions may differ.]
