## Introduccion

GeoSystems Analysis (GeoSystems) lleva a cabo estudios de cierre para el Botadero Este de la Compania Minera Antamina S.A. (Antamina). SRK Consulting (Argentina) S.A. analiza la estabilidad de los paneles de prueba que componen las alternativas de coberturas a emplazar sobre el Botadero Este.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The use of test panels to evaluate cover alternatives for waste rock dump closure is consistent with established mine closure practices. Test panels are standard methodology in cover system design, allowing field evaluation of cover system performance before final closure design [WEB:https://geosyntheticsmagazine.com/2013/06/01/evaluations-of-engineered-cover-systems-for-mine-waste-rock-and-tailings/][WEB:https://www.qmrc.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0029/395354/mine-waste-cover-trials-case-study-review.pdf]. The involvement of specialized geotechnical consultancy firms like SRK in stability analysis for mine closure is typical industry practice. However, specific details about the Antamina East Waste Rock Dump and these particular test panels could not be independently verified from external sources.]

### Objetivo

El objetivo del presente estudio consiste en evaluar la estabilidad fisica de las coberturas segun las configuraciones propuestas bajo condiciones estaticas y dinamicas. El desempeno en condiciones reales de las coberturas en estos paneles de prueba sera evaluado para realizar los ajustes necesarios de cara al diseno definitivo de cierre.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Evaluating physical stability of cover systems under both static and dynamic conditions is standard practice in geotechnical analysis for mine closure. Industry guidelines explicitly require assessment of cover stability under various loading conditions [WEB:https://www.lopproject.com/mine-waste-guidelines/]. The use of test panel performance to inform final closure design aligns with established methodologies where trial covers are monitored to evaluate performance before implementing full-scale closure designs [WEB:https://hatarilabs.com/ih-en/soil-cover-system-design-for-mine-waste-dumps]. The iterative approach of testing, evaluating, and adjusting before final design is documented as best practice in mine waste closure [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/237562266_FINDINGS_OF_INTERNATIONAL_REVIEW_OF_SOIL_COVER_DESIGN_AND_CONSTRUCTION_PRACTICES_FOR_MINE_WASTE_CLOSURE].]

### Alcances

Este es un estudio de gabinete de ingenieria a nivel basico, basado en la informacion suministrada por GeoSystems y Antamina. El analisis y validacion de esta informacion esta fuera del alcance del servicio de SRK.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The description of this as a "basic level desktop engineering study" is consistent with typical phased approaches in geotechnical consulting where preliminary analyses are conducted based on client-provided information before more detailed investigations. The explicit exclusion of data validation from the scope is a standard limitation statement in engineering reports. However, this scope limitation means the analysis quality depends critically on the accuracy of input data provided by others, which is a recognized constraint in geotechnical practice but raises questions about verification rigor.]

El alcance del servicio se limita a proporcionar recomendaciones para la caracterizacion de los materiales de cobertura, revisar la amenaza sismica y realizar un analisis de desempeno de las alternativas de cobertura frente a escenarios estaticos y dinamicos. La estabilidad del botadero de base se analiza en forma aislada de manera de cuantificar la seguridad del sistema de soporte.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The scope items listed represent standard components of geotechnical stability analysis for mine closure. Material characterization of cover materials is fundamental to cover design [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/346730391_Geotechnical_characterization_of_mine_waste_rock_Characterisation_geotechnique_de_debris_rocheux_miniers]. Seismic hazard review is essential for dynamic stability assessment in mining regions with seismic activity [WEB:https://umbrex.com/resources/industry-analyses/how-to-analyze-a-metals-mining-company/seismic-and-geotechnical-stability-assessment-of-mine-sites/]. Performance analysis under static and dynamic scenarios is required for comprehensive stability evaluation [WEB:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S259012302501432X]. Analyzing the base waste dump stability separately to quantify the support system safety is a sound engineering approach consistent with limit equilibrium and numerical modeling practices [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/371908326_Probabilistic_Slope_Stability_Analysis_of_Coal_Mine_Waste_Rock_Dump].]

El efecto de la erosion de la cobertura y el impacto de deformaciones diferidas en el botadero y su erosion quedan excluidos de este analisis.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Excluding cover erosion and long-term deformations from the analysis scope represents a significant limitation given that these factors are recognized as important considerations in long-term stability and cover performance. Industry guidelines note that erosion control and long-term movement are vital in waste dump design and management, particularly for closure [WEB:https://www.lopproject.com/mine-waste-guidelines/][WEB:https://www.srk.com/en/publications/waste-rock-dump-management-and-stability-evaluation]. The exclusion of time-dependent deformations may be appropriate for an initial basic-level study but would typically need to be addressed in final closure design. This limitation should be clearly understood when interpreting the study results.]
