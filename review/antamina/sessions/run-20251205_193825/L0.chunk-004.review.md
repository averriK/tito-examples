## Recomendaciones

Las recomendaciones presentadas a continuacion se fundamentan tanto en los resultados del analisis deterministico y de sensibilidad como en los hallazgos derivados de la evaluacion probabilistica de estabilidad.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Basing recommendations on integrated results from deterministic, sensitivity, and probabilistic analyses is best practice in geotechnical engineering. This multi-faceted approach provides a more comprehensive basis for design recommendations than any single analysis method alone [WEB:https://cdnsciencepub.com/doi/10.1139/t10-044].]

El analisis desarrollado sobre las alternativas de coberturas y sus respectivas condiciones de estabilidad ha permitido identificar que la mayoria de las alternativas evaluadas cumplen adecuadamente con los criterios minimos de seguridad deterministicos, salvo en ciertos casos donde la configuracion de material y pendiente resulta mas exigente: el suelo organico en taludes mas inclinados y los suelos finos en pendientes intermedias.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: This summary accurately reflects the analysis results presented in the previous section, where organic soil on steep slopes (1.7:1) and fine soils (clay) on intermediate slopes (2.2:1) showed mean factors of safety below the 1.30 criterion. However, the characterization that "most alternatives adequately meet deterministic safety criteria" depends on whether marginal compliance (mean FoS slightly above 1.30 but with probability of falling below) is considered adequate. Given the parameter uncertainties acknowledged throughout the document, this optimistic characterization may understate the design challenges for the problematic configurations.]

A partir de ello, se profundizo el estudio incorporando un enfoque probabilistico, que permitio evaluar la influencia de la variabilidad de los parametros geotecnicos. El analisis probabilistico permitio estimar la probabilidad de falla (PoF) de cada alternativa, aportando una vision mas realista del desempeno esperado bajo condiciones de incertidumbre.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The incorporation of probabilistic analysis to evaluate parameter variability and estimate probability of failure provides a more complete assessment than deterministic analysis alone, particularly when parameter uncertainty is significant [WEB:https://researchrepository.wvu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1993&context=etd][WEB:https://risk-engineering.org/notebook/monte-carlo-slope-stability.html]. This approach is consistent with modern reliability-based geotechnical design practice.]

En la mayoria de las configuraciones evaluadas, la PoF resulto menor al 1%, confirmando un nivel de seguridad elevado. Las unicas excepciones se observaron en coincidencia con los casos en donde el analisis deterministico determino FoS menores a los requeridos. En estos casos, la PoF se encuentra entre el 5 y el 10%. Estas probabilidades pueden considerarse aceptables dentro del criterio de diseno adoptado para consecuencias de falla moderadas, conforme a lo recomendado por guias internacionales [@Hawley2017]. La definicion final del nivel de consecuencia y, por ende, la probabilidad de falla admisible debe ser determinada y asumida en conjunto con Compania Minera Antamina de acuerdo con sus estandares internos, su nivel de tolerancia al riesgo y los objetivos de manejo ambiental y operacional del sitio.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The summary accurately reflects the probabilistic results showing most configurations with PoF < 1% and two cases with PoF of 5-10%. The statement that 5-10% PoF "can be considered acceptable" for moderate failure consequences is consistent with the design criteria established earlier based on Hawley2017, though this reference cannot be independently verified. The emphasis that final consequence level and acceptable failure probability must be jointly determined with the mine owner is appropriate, as these are ultimately risk management decisions [WEB:https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3263/11/8/319]. However, PoF values of 5-10% are relatively high compared to typical practice for permanent engineered structures, and acceptance depends critically on the validity of the "moderate" consequence classification and stakeholder risk tolerance. For test panels with monitoring and remediation capability, this may be acceptable; for final closure, more conservative criteria would typically apply.]

En funcion de los resultados integrados de los distintos enfoques de analisis, se establece el siguiente ranking de desempeno para las alternativas de cobertura, ordenado segun los criterios de estabilidad superficial alcanzados en los distintos escenarios evaluados:

1. Coberturas multicapas con materiales compuestos (arenas limpias, mezclas de relaves, desmonte, geomembranas y Trisoplast): muestran el mayor margen de seguridad y respuesta estable ante solicitaciones estaticas.
2. Coberturas con arenas: mantienen valores de seguridad satisfactorios y presentan baja sensibilidad ante variaciones en la geometria del talud.
3. Coberturas con suelos finos o arcillas: el desempeno es adecuado solo cuando se emplean materiales seleccionados y pendientes moderadas.
4. Coberturas con suelo organico en pendientes pronunciadas: muestran los menores factores de seguridad y probabilidades de falla mas altas, requiriendo optimizacion en su diseno y control en su ejecucion. El nivel de riesgo se encuadra dentro de los margenes definidos por las guias y criterios adoptados, pero debe confirmarse segun la gestion de riesgo interna.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The performance ranking is consistent with the tabulated results showing: (1) Multi-layer covers with composite materials (Types D, I, J) achieved mean FoS of 1.32-1.51 and very low PoF < 0.4%, (2) Sand drainage layers contributed to higher stability in multi-layer systems, (3) Clay-based Cover B showed adequate but marginal performance (mean FoS 1.18, PoF 5.13%) only on moderate slopes, (4) Simple organic soil Cover A on steep slopes showed lowest performance (mean FoS 1.17, PoF 8.1%). This ranking appropriately reflects relative performance and identifies the need for design optimization and execution control for the weakest configurations. The caveat that risk level must be confirmed according to internal risk management is prudent. The ranking is consistent with general principles that multi-layer covers with engineered materials typically outperform simple single-layer soil covers [WEB:https://geosyntheticsmagazine.com/2013/06/01/evaluations-of-engineered-cover-systems-for-mine-waste-rock-and-tailings/].]

En el caso particular de la arcilla, se recomienda su uso primordialmente en aquellas situaciones donde las condiciones de impermeabilidad lo demanden expresamente, dado que sus propiedades pueden variar considerablemente con el tiempo y las condiciones de humedad. Para maximizar el desempeno de las arcillas (y materiales similares), se sugiere realizar ensayos de Casagrande para determinar su indice de plasticidad. De este modo, podra seleccionarse preferentemente para los taludes expuestos el material con menor plasticidad (idealmente indice de plasticidad por debajo de 20), reservando los suelos de mayor plasticidad para zonas de bermas donde sea posible aplicar una compactacion controlada.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The recommendation to use clay primarily where impermeability is specifically required, given time-dependent property changes with moisture, is sound engineering practice. High plasticity clays are particularly susceptible to swelling, shrinkage, and strength loss with moisture cycles, which can affect long-term performance [WEB:https://www.scdot.org/content/dam/scdot-legacy/business/pdf/geotech/2022-by-chapter/Chapter07-Geomechanics-12132021.pdf]. The recommendation to perform Casagrande liquid limit tests to determine plasticity index and select low plasticity material (PI < 20) for slopes while reserving high plasticity material for berms is appropriate. Materials with PI < 15-20 are more compatible with compaction and exhibit less problematic behavior than high plasticity clays [WEB:http://geotechdata.info/parameter/angle-of-friction]. This recommendation addresses the wide plasticity range (PI 13-96) identified in the materials characterization and the marginal stability results for clay on slopes.]

Finalmente, se recomienda contar con una caracterizacion de los volumenes de cada uno de los materiales de cobertura, lo cual permitira una planificacion de la ejecucion y una optimizacion de recursos en funcion de la seguridad requerida. Considerando que estos taludes piloto permitiran observar y medir el desempeno real de las alternativas, sumado a los factores de seguridad reducidos y la amplia variabilidad de los materiales empleados, se recomienda su ejecucion acompanada por un riguroso monitoreo en campo. Esto permitira recolectar informacion clave para futuras etapas de diseno y realizar ajustes en caso de desviaciones respecto a los objetivos previstos.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The recommendations for material volume characterization, rigorous field monitoring, and data collection to inform future design are essential best practices for test panel programs. Given the acknowledged limitations of this study (low parameter confidence, rejected site-specific test results, lack of independent validation, marginal stability results, and high material variability), field monitoring is critical to validate design assumptions and allow for corrective action if performance deviates from predictions [WEB:https://www.qmrc.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0029/395354/mine-waste-cover-trials-case-study-review.pdf]. Test panel methodology specifically relies on field performance observation to evaluate cover alternatives before final closure design [WEB:https://geosyntheticsmagazine.com/2013/06/01/evaluations-of-engineered-cover-systems-for-mine-waste-rock-and-tailings/]. The explicit recognition that reduced factors of safety and wide material variability necessitate rigorous monitoring is appropriate engineering judgment. This recommendation is essential for the project to achieve its stated objective of informing final closure design.]
