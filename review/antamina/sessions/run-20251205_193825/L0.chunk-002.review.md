#### Suelo organico

El suelo organico disponible proviene de acopios existentes en el sitio y presenta una granulometria variable, con contenido de finos entre 22% y 91%, y un contenido de gravas que varia entre 0 y 35%. Su plasticidad es moderada a alta, con indices de plasticidad entre 19 y 26 y limites liquidos de 56% a 63%.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The described characteristics of organic soil with variable grain size distribution (22-91% fines, 0-35% gravel), moderate to high plasticity (plasticity index 19-26, liquid limit 56-63%) represent a heterogeneous material typical of stockpiled topsoil at mine sites. The wide ranges in properties indicate significant material variability that would affect behavior and necessitate careful characterization for design purposes. The plasticity characteristics described are consistent with silty to clayey soils of moderate to high plasticity.]

El estudio preliminar de alternativas de cierre [@GeoSystems2024] indica la presencia de dos tipos de materiales: un suelo organico fino, con un angulo de friccion interna de 25 grados y cohesion de 1 kPa, y otro de naturaleza mas gruesa, con un angulo de friccion de 30 grados y cohesion de 0.5 kPa. Los pesos unitarios adoptados son de 16.5 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ y 17 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$, respectivamente. Estos valores de peso unitario y cohesion se encuentran dentro del rango esperado [@JCSS2006]. Asimismo, de acuerdo con [@Sorensen2013], los angulos de friccion utilizados se encuentran dentro del rango esperable para suelos con los indices de plasticidad reportados, estimandose entre 23 y 30 grados, para indices de 26 y 19 respectivamente.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The friction angles of 25-30 degrees and low cohesions of 0.5-1 kPa for organic soils are within typical ranges for such materials. Research on friction angle-plasticity relationships shows that for clays and silty soils, friction angle generally decreases with increasing plasticity index [WEB:http://geotechdata.info/parameter/angle-of-friction]. For plasticity indices of 19-26, friction angles in the range of 23-30 degrees are plausible though on the higher end of typical values. The unit weights of 16.5-17 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ are reasonable for organic soils. However, without access to the referenced JCSS2006 and Sorensen2013 sources, and given the stated lack of reliable site-specific test data, these parameter selections have moderate uncertainty.]

#### Arcillas

Las arcillas propuestas como material de cubierta presentan una alta variabilidad mineralogica, mostrando limites liquidos entre 33 y 125 y un indice de plasticidad entre 13 y 96, lo que indica la presencia de minerales de alta plasticidad (como montmorillonita). Para su utilizacion, en el estudio preliminar [@GeoSystems2024] se plantea la compactacion al 95% de densidad maxima segun el ensayo Proctor estandar. Sin embargo, estos suelos exhiben comportamientos muy diversos segun su plasticidad: los suelos con indice de plasticidad menor a 15 o 20 probablemente puedan compactarse adecuadamente, pero los materiales con plasticidad mayor son incompatibles con ese grado de compactacion y, de ser necesario su empleo en zonas inclinadas, deberan ser estabilizados, tal como se senala en las recomendaciones. Dado que la estabilizacion puede ser antieconomica, se sugiere reservar los materiales de mayor plasticidad para bermas y sectores horizontales donde los requerimientos de compacidad y resistencia sean menores.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The description of high variability in clay mineralogy with liquid limits of 33-125 and plasticity indices of 13-96 indicates the presence of highly plastic clay minerals including montmorillonite, which is known for high plasticity and swelling behavior. The observation that achieving 95% standard Proctor compaction is problematic for highly plastic clays (PI > 15-20) is well-established in geotechnical engineering practice. High plasticity clays are difficult to compact and prone to swelling and shrinkage with moisture changes, making them unsuitable for use on slopes without stabilization [WEB:https://www.scdot.org/content/dam/scdot-legacy/business/pdf/geotech/2022-by-chapter/Chapter07-Geomechanics-12132021.pdf]. The recommendation to reserve high plasticity materials for berms and horizontal areas where strength requirements are lower is sound engineering practice.]

Para las condiciones de estabilidad pico se utilizan parametros representativos de una arcilla compactada: angulo de friccion de 22 grados y cohesion de 5 kPa; para grandes deformaciones, los valores son de 22 grados y 2 kPa, respectivamente.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The specified parameters for compacted clay (friction angle 22 degrees, cohesion 5 kPa at peak, 2 kPa at large deformation) fall within typical ranges for compacted clay. Reference data shows that high plasticity clay (CH) typically has friction angle around 22 degrees and cohesion of 25 kPa, though the cohesion values used here (2-5 kPa) are considerably lower, representing weak clay [WEB:https://www.ecorisq.org/docs/USCS_soilclasses.pdf]. The distinction between peak and residual (large deformation) strength is appropriate, with the reduction in cohesion from 5 to 2 kPa representing post-peak softening behavior common in clays. However, given the extreme variability in clay properties described (PI 13-96) and lack of independent validation, confidence in these single-value parameters is moderate.]

Se reconoce que estos valores pueden variar en funcion del tiempo debido a procesos de hinchamiento o ablandamiento. El peso unitario considerado es 17 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$. Estos valores de peso unitario y cohesion se encuentran dentro del rango esperado [@JCSS2006]. Asimismo, de acuerdo con [@Sorensen2013], los angulos de friccion adoptados se encuentran dentro del rango esperable para arcillas con los indices de plasticidad reportados, estimandose angulos de friccion entre 17 y 27 grados, para indices entre 96 y 13 respectivamente.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The acknowledgment that these parameters may vary over time due to swelling or softening is important for long-term closure design, as high plasticity clays with montmorillonite are particularly susceptible to moisture-related volume changes. The unit weight of 17 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ is reasonable for compacted clay. The statement that friction angles of 17-27 degrees are expected for clays with plasticity indices of 13-96 aligns with general trends showing friction angle decreasing with increasing plasticity [WEB:http://geotechdata.info/parameter/angle-of-friction]. However, without access to the cited references and given the lack of site-specific validated data, confidence remains moderate.]

#### Arenas

La arena utilizada en las cubiertas corresponde a arena limpia colocada con una compactacion baja o nula, aunque una ligera compactacion adicional puede ocurrir tras la colocacion del suelo organico superior. Estas arenas aseguran una adecuada permeabilidad y son tambien utilizadas como material de cama bajo geomembranas. El material se caracteriza como no cohesivo, tipico de arenas para drenaje, y su resistencia se basa unicamente en la friccion interna. El estudio preliminar de alternativas de cierre [@GeoSystems2024] considera un angulo de friccion de 34 grados, cohesion nula, peso unitario humedo de 18.5 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ y saturado de 20.8 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$. Sin embargo, SRK considera que este angulo de friccion puede resultar elevado para un material colocado sin un control de compactacion y recomienda adoptar un valor de 31 grados, aproximadamente igual al angulo de friccion interna a volumen constante [@EPRI1990].

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The characterization of drainage sand as clean, cohesionless material with low or no compaction is consistent with drainage layer design in cover systems. SRK's concern that 34 degrees friction angle is high for loosely placed sand and the recommendation to use 31 degrees (approximately the critical state friction angle) is sound engineering judgment. For clean sands, the critical state (constant volume) friction angle typically ranges from 30-35 degrees depending on grain characteristics [WEB:http://geotechdata.info/parameter/angle-of-friction]. The unit weights specified (18.5 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ moist, 20.8 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ saturated) are typical for loose to medium dense clean sand. The adoption of a conservative friction angle for uncompacted material is prudent for stability analysis.]

#### Trisoplast

El Trisoplast es un material compuesto por arena, bentonita y un polimero, disenado para tener baja permeabilidad y cierta resistencia estructural. La interaccion del polimero con la bentonita y la arena genera una estructura tipo gel, aportando cohesion al ensamblado granular. El material es compactado al 95% de su densidad relativa y se emplea como barrera en la cubierta. Los parametros resistentes del material han sido provistos por el proveedor y han sido verificados mediante ensayos de laboratorio en otros estudios de analisis de estabilidad publicados [@Behrens2008].

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Trisoplast and similar polymer-modified bentonite-sand composites are proprietary engineered materials used as low-permeability barriers in cover systems. The description of gel-structure formation from polymer-bentonite interaction providing cohesion to a granular assembly is consistent with the behavior of such composite materials. However, relying on manufacturer-provided parameters without independent site-specific testing is a limitation. The reference to verification in published stability studies (Behrens2008) provides some validation, but without access to this reference and confirmation that testing was performed on material from the same production lot and placed under similar conditions, confidence remains moderate.]

Para el analisis se consideran un angulo de friccion interna de 30 grados y una cohesion de 10 kPa, junto a un peso unitario de 16.6 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$, valores que reflejan su comportamiento de tipo "arena cementada" con cohesion aportada por su estructura interna.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The parameters of 30 degrees friction angle and 10 kPa cohesion for Trisoplast reflect "cemented sand" behavior, which is conceptually appropriate for a polymer-modified bentonite-sand composite. The cohesion of 10 kPa represents the contribution from the gel structure. The unit weight of 16.6 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ is reasonable for a compacted sand-bentonite-polymer mixture. However, without independent verification of these manufacturer-provided parameters or access to the Behrens2008 reference, and considering that such composite materials can exhibit variable behavior depending on placement conditions and aging, confidence remains moderate.]

#### Mezcla de relaves y roca triturada (commingled tailings)

Las mezclas de relaves y roca triturada corresponden a combinaciones de materiales con granulometria predominantemente gruesa, usando proporciones de entre 4:1 y 1:1 (roca de desmonte:relave), y considerando relaves filtrados, en pasta y ciclonados. Los parametros resistentes del material han sido determinados por [@Golder2021] a traves de ensayos triaxiales, los cuales muestran que bajo 100 kPa de confinamiento el material se comporta como un material granular puro en las condiciones del sistema de cubierta, con cohesion despreciable y angulos de friccion internos entre 41.5 y 43 grados. El peso unitario estimado es de 19.0 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$, y la capacidad de resistencia proviene de la friccion entre particulas de roca y relave.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Commingled tailings-waste rock mixtures are used in some mining operations as cover or structural fill materials. The description of behavior as pure granular material with negligible cohesion and friction angles of 41.5-43 degrees at 100 kPa confining stress is plausible for coarse granular mixtures. Triaxial testing is the appropriate method for characterizing such materials. However, friction angles in the 41-43 degree range are at the high end of typical values and would require good particle interlocking and angular particles. Without access to the Golder2021 testing report and given that material behavior is highly sensitive to mixing ratio, particle characteristics, and placement conditions, confidence in applying these parameters to the current project is moderate. The unit weight of 19.0 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ is reasonable for such mixtures.]

Para el analisis se considera un angulo de friccion con un valor medio de 42 grados. Si bien este valor medio puede considerarse algo elevado para este tipo de suelos, especialmente en las mezclas con proporciones 1:1, en el analisis de sensibilidad se evaluaron tambien casos con un angulo de 37.8 grados, valor que resulta mas adecuado para dichas condiciones.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The acknowledgment that 42 degrees friction angle may be "somewhat high" for these materials, especially for 1:1 mixtures, and the inclusion of sensitivity analysis with a lower value of 37.8 degrees shows appropriate engineering caution. Friction angles above 40 degrees typically require dense, well-interlocked angular particles [WEB:http://geotechdata.info/parameter/angle-of-friction]. The sensitivity analysis approach is sound for addressing parameter uncertainty. However, the mean value of 42 degrees remains at the high end and may be optimistic for the actual as-placed material, particularly in looser or more tailings-rich zones.]

#### Relleno de desmonte (waste rock)

El relleno de desmonte esta conformado por material de roca ocupando un rango amplio de granulometria, presentando una envolvente de resistencia no lineal y dependencia del nivel de confinamiento. Para los analisis de estabilidad a baja profundidad, se adopta una funcion de resistencia no lineal definida por Antamina, sin presencia de cohesion, respaldado por ensayos previos y observaciones de campo [@Piteau2022]. Su peso unitario es de 19.6 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The description of waste rock as having wide grain size distribution, nonlinear strength envelope, and confinement-dependent behavior is characteristic of rockfill and waste rock materials. The use of a nonlinear strength function without cohesion is appropriate for such materials, as their strength derives primarily from friction and interlocking that varies with confinement level [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/346730391_Geotechnical_characterization_of_mine_waste_rock_Characterisation_geotechnique_de_debris_rocheux_miniers]. The unit weight of 19.6 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ is typical for waste rock. However, the analysis relies on a site-specific nonlinear function "defined by Antamina" from the Piteau2022 report, which cannot be independently verified. The appropriateness of these parameters for shallow depth stability analysis of cover systems depends on whether the function was calibrated for the relevant low-stress conditions.]

#### Interfaz geomembrana y arena

La interaccion entre la geomembrana texturizada de HDPE/LLDPE y la arena drenante determina la resistencia por friccion y adhesion en la interfaz, influyendo en la estabilidad ante deslizamientos superficiales. El angulo de friccion interna de la interfaz es 31 grados y la adhesion es 1 kPa en condiciones de esfuerzo pico; en condiciones de grandes deformaciones, el angulo de friccion disminuye a 26 grados, manteniendose la adhesion en 1 kPa. Estos valores se han determinado mediante ensayos especificos y representan un comportamiento intermedio entre el material granular puro y la interfaz de materiales sinteticos [@Howell2016]. Para el diseno ejecutivo, estos valores asumidos deberan ser validados o ajustados de acuerdo con las especificaciones del proveedor.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Interface friction between geomembranes and sand is critical for cover stability on slopes. The specified peak interface friction angle of 31 degrees and residual of 26 degrees, with adhesion of 1 kPa, falls within documented ranges. Research shows that textured HDPE geomembranes can mobilize 65-90% or more of the friction angle of the contacting sand depending on surface roughness [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/237378082_Geomembrane_interface_friction][WEB:https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1944/13/7/1672]. For clean sand with friction angle around 30-35 degrees, an interface friction of 26-31 degrees (75-90% of sand friction) is plausible for textured geomembrane. The recognition that sand-geomembrane interface is often the weakest link in multilayer cover systems is well-documented [WEB:https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40891-016-0063-5]. However, the statement that final design should validate these values with manufacturer specifications is important, as interface behavior is highly product-specific.]

### Criterios de diseno

Para la evaluacion de la estabilidad se siguieron los lineamientos adoptados por [@Piteau2022] y [@Stantec2024], basados en los criterios definidos en [@Golder2016]. Los factores de seguridad ($\mathrm{FoS}$) minimos recomendados se indican en la siguiente tabla:

| Condicion | $\mathrm{FoS}_{\mathrm{min}}$ |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Estabilidad superficial de coberturas. Condicion estatica | 1.30 |
| Estabilidad global de taludes. Condicion estatica | 1.50 |
| Condicion de carga extrema (sismo). Pseudo-estatico | 1.00 |

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The specified minimum factors of safety are consistent with typical design criteria for mine closure. Static FoS of 1.3 for superficial cover stability and 1.5 for global slope stability are common acceptance criteria [WEB:https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10706-023-02541-2]. Research shows that FoS values exceeding minimums of 1.1-1.5 are appropriate for stability under different loading conditions [WEB:https://www.hindawi.com/journals/ace/2018/6835709/]. The pseudo-static seismic FoS criterion of 1.0 is a common threshold, though this represents a minimum acceptable value where permanent deformations would be expected. La adopcion de este criterio permite una evaluacion mas realista de la seguridad, incorporando la incertidumbre inherente a los parametros empleados en el analisis de estabilidad.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The use of probability of failure (PoF) as a complementary acceptance criterion alongside deterministic factor of safety is consistent with modern reliability-based design approaches in geotechnical engineering. ### Metodos de analisis

#### Estabilidad superficial de cobertura

La estabilidad de las diferentes alternativas para el sistema de cubierta fue evaluada utilizando metodos de estabilidad de taludes por equilibrio limite. En este tipo de analisis, se compara la resistencia de los materiales del suelo frente a las fuerzas gravitacionales que pueden provocar inestabilidad en el talud.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Limit equilibrium methods are the standard approach for slope stability analysis and are appropriate for evaluating cover system stability [WEB:https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slope_stability_analysis]. The fundamental concept of comparing soil resistance to gravitational driving forces correctly describes limit equilibrium analysis.]

Se utilizo el procedimiento del talud infinito para el analisis de la cobertura dado que, cuando la cobertura es de un espesor reducido en comparacion con la longitud de la pendiente, se puede suponer que el talud es "infinito", lo que implica que la resistencia que aporta el pie del talud es minima y se puede despreciar. Este metodo considera que cualquier posible deslizamiento ocurre de manera paralela a la cara de la pendiente, lo que simplifica considerablemente el analisis.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The infinite slope method is the standard analytical approach for shallow cover stability analysis when cover thickness is small relative to slope length. The description correctly explains that this method assumes failure parallel to the slope surface and that toe resistance is negligible, which are the fundamental assumptions of infinite slope analysis [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/315474880_Performance-Based_Slope_Stability_Analysis_and_the_Pseudo-Static_Factor_of_Safety]. This method is widely used and well-validated for cover system stability evaluation [WEB:http://fl-nzgs-media.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/2022/06/Bray-2011-Pseudostatic-slope-stability-procedure-paper-3.pdf].]

Esta metodologia es aplicable sin caer en supuestos conservadores siempre que la altura vertical del talud sea al menos 30 veces mayor que el espesor de la cobertura.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The criterion that slope height should be at least 30 times the cover thickness for infinite slope assumptions to be valid is a commonly cited rule of thumb in geotechnical practice. For an 80-meter slope with covers ranging from approximately 0.4 to 1.0 meter thick, the height-to-thickness ratio ranges from 80 to 200, well exceeding the 30:1 threshold, so the infinite slope method is appropriate. However, the specific threshold value and the statement about "conservative assumptions" could not be verified from external sources. The criterion is plausible but may vary in literature.]

Para llevar a cabo los calculos deterministicos, se utilizo una hoja de calculo en Excel, donde se detallaron los datos intermedios y resultados obtenidos. ^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Use of spreadsheet calculations for infinite slope analysis is standard practice as the method involves relatively simple closed-form equations. Performing deterministic analysis with mean parameter values plus sensitivity analysis by varying individual parameters is an appropriate approach to assess parameter influence on stability [WEB:https://cdnsciencepub.com/doi/10.1139/t10-044].]

De manera complementaria, se realizo una evaluacion probabilistica de la estabilidad de taludes, con el objetivo de incorporar explicitamente la incertidumbre asociada a la variabilidad de los parametros resistentes de los materiales de las coberturas.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The Monte Carlo simulation approach with 10,000 samples is appropriate for probabilistic slope stability analysis.
