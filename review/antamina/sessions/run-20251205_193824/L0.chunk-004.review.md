## Recomendaciones

Las recomendaciones presentadas a continuacion se fundamentan tanto en los resultados del analisis deterministico y de sensibilidad como en los hallazgos derivados de la evaluacion probabilistica de estabilidad.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: This statement accurately describes the basis for the recommendations as integrating multiple analytical approaches (deterministic, sensitivity analysis, and probabilistic evaluation) presented in the preceding sections. This multi-method approach is consistent with modern geotechnical design practice for risk-informed decision making [WEB:https://www.nzgs.org/libraries/risk-based-approach-to-factor-of-safety-selection/]. The integration of deterministic and probabilistic results provides a more comprehensive understanding of stability and uncertainty than either approach alone.]

El analisis desarrollado sobre las alternativas de coberturas y sus respectivas condiciones de estabilidad ha permitido identificar que la mayoria de las alternativas evaluadas cumplen adecuadamente con los criterios minimos de seguridad deterministicos, salvo en ciertos casos donde la configuracion de material y pendiente resulta mas exigente: el suelo organico en taludes mas inclinados y los suelos finos en pendientes intermedias.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: This summary accurately reflects the results presented in the Verification section. The analysis showed that most configurations met the $\mathrm{FoS} \geq 1.3$ criterion, with exceptions for organic topsoil on 1.7:1 slopes (mean $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.17$) and clay on 2.2:1 slopes (mean $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.18$). The characterization of these as "more demanding" configurations is appropriate given the combination of low-strength materials and relatively steep slopes. The statement accurately summarizes the key findings regarding which material-geometry combinations present stability challenges.]

A partir de ello, se profundizo el estudio incorporando un enfoque probabilistico, que permitio evaluar la influencia de la variabilidad de los parametros geotecnicos. El analisis probabilistico permitio estimar la probabilidad de falla (PoF) de cada alternativa, aportando una vision mas realista del desempeno esperado bajo condiciones de incertidumbre.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: This statement correctly describes the methodological progression from deterministic to probabilistic analysis. The probabilistic approach (Monte Carlo simulation with parameter distributions as described in the Methods section) explicitly accounts for geotechnical parameter uncertainty and provides probability of failure estimates. This approach is recognized as providing a more realistic assessment of performance under uncertainty compared to single-value deterministic analysis [WEB:https://klohn.com/blog/geotechnical-factor-of-safety-and-risk/]. The characterization of probabilistic methods as offering a "more realistic view" is supported by geotechnical reliability literature.]

En la mayoria de las configuraciones evaluadas, la PoF resulto menor al 1%, confirmando un nivel de seguridad elevado. Las unicas excepciones se observaron en coincidencia con los casos en donde el analisis deterministico determino FoS menores a los requeridos. En estos casos, la PoF se encuentra entre el 5 y el 10%. Estas probabilidades pueden considerarse aceptables dentro del criterio de diseno adoptado para consecuencias de falla moderadas, conforme a lo recomendado por guias internacionales [@Hawley2017]. La definicion final del nivel de consecuencia y, por ende, la probabilidad de falla admisible debe ser determinada y asumida en conjunto con Compania Minera Antamina de acuerdo con sus estandares internos, su nivel de tolerancia al riesgo y los objetivos de manejo ambiental y operacional del sitio.^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The summary of results is accurate: six of ten configurations showed $\mathrm{PoF} < 1\%$, and elevated $\mathrm{PoF}$ (5.13% and 8.10%) occurred where mean $\mathrm{FoS} < 1.3$, demonstrating consistency between deterministic and probabilistic assessments. The reference to Hawley 2017 (Hawley and Cunning, "Guidelines for Mine Waste Dump and Stockpile Design") for acceptance of 10% $\mathrm{PoF}$ for moderate consequences is generally consistent with risk-based design frameworks in mine geotechnics [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Waste-Dump-and-Stockpile-Stability-Rating-Classification-Hawley-and-Cunning-2017_fig2_384288154][WEB:https://www.routledge.com/Guidelines-for-Mine-Waste-Dump-and-Stockpile-Design/Hawley-Cunning/p/book/9781138197312]. However, the specific 10% threshold depends on consequence classification and data confidence. The recommendation to jointly determine acceptability with the client based on their internal standards and risk tolerance is appropriate and aligns with best practice for risk-informed design. The 5-10% $\mathrm{PoF}$ values are notably higher than typical targets for permanent mine closure structures but may be acceptable for pilot test panels with monitoring and remediation capability.]

En funcion de los resultados integrados de los distintos enfoques de analisis, se establece el siguiente ranking de desempeno para las alternativas de cobertura, ordenado segun los criterios de estabilidad superficial alcanzados en los distintos escenarios evaluados:^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The establishment of a performance ranking based on integrated results from multiple analysis approaches is a logical synthesis of the findings. This ranking methodology provides practical guidance for design optimization and material selection based on stability performance.]

1. Coberturas multicapas con materiales compuestos (arenas limpias, mezclas de relaves, desmonte, geomembranas y Trisoplast): muestran el mayor margen de seguridad y respuesta estable ante solicitaciones estaticas.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The results table supports this ranking. Cover alternatives with multi-layer composite systems showed the highest mean $\mathrm{FoS}$ values: Alternative J with commingled tailings (mean $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.51$), Alternative D with geomembrane (mean $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.37$), and Alternative I with Trisoplast (mean $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.32$). All of these configurations achieved mean $\mathrm{FoS} > 1.3$ and very low $\mathrm{PoF}$ ($< 0.4\%$), confirming high safety margins and stable response. The superior performance is attributable to the higher strength parameters of these materials compared to organic topsoil and clay.]

2. Coberturas con arenas: mantienen valores de seguridad satisfactorios y presentan baja sensibilidad ante variaciones en la geometria del talud.^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Sand layers were components of Alternatives B, D, I, and J as drainage layers beneath other materials. The referenced "satisfactory safety values and low sensitivity to slope geometry" are partially supported by the results, though sand was not evaluated as a standalone cover alternative in the results table. The statement appears to be based on the general behavior of sand materials (non-cohesive, angle-of-friction-dependent stability) and their role in successful multi-layer systems. Cohesionless materials like sand do show relatively predictable stability behavior across slope geometries when properly characterized. However, independent evidence for this specific ranking position is limited by the absence of sand-only alternatives in the test program.]

3. Coberturas con suelos finos o arcillas: el desempeno es adecuado solo cuando se emplean materiales seleccionados y pendientes moderadas.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The results for Alternative B (clay) demonstrate the conditional nature of clay cover performance. On 2.2:1 slopes, clay showed mean $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.18$ with $\mathrm{PoF} = 5.13\%$, falling below the $\mathrm{FoS} \geq 1.3$ criterion. This confirms that adequate performance requires "selected materials and moderate slopes" as stated. Clay materials are known to exhibit high variability in properties depending on plasticity, mineralogy, and compaction [WEB:https://www.soilconnect.com/blog/understanding-plasticity-index-what-it-means-for-soil-performance]. The wide plasticity range (PI 13-96) noted for available clays in the Materials section further supports the need for material selection. The ranking reflects the inherent challenges of using fine-grained cohesive soils on slopes.]

4. Coberturas con suelo organico en pendientes pronunciadas: muestran los menores factores de seguridad y probabilidades de falla mas altas, requiriendo optimizacion en su diseno y control en su ejecucion. El nivel de riesgo se encuadra dentro de los margenes definidos por las guias y criterios adoptados, pero debe confirmarse segun la gestion de riesgo interna.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Alternative A (organic topsoil) on 1.7:1 slopes consistently showed the lowest mean $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.17$ and highest $\mathrm{PoF} = 8.10\%$ across Sections 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6. This unambiguously confirms the statement that organic soil on steep slopes exhibits the lowest factors of safety and highest failure probabilities. The need for design optimization and execution control is justified given the marginal stability ($\mathrm{FoS} < 1.3$). The acknowledgment that risk levels fall within adopted guideline margins (referencing the 10% $\mathrm{PoF}$ threshold discussed earlier) while requiring internal risk management confirmation is appropriate. The relatively low strength parameters of organic soil (friction angle 25-30 degrees, cohesion 0.5-1 kPa) combined with the steep 1.7:1 slope geometry explain the poor performance ranking.]

En el caso particular de la arcilla, se recomienda su uso primordialmente en aquellas situaciones donde las condiciones de impermeabilidad lo demanden expresamente, dado que sus propiedades pueden variar considerablemente con el tiempo y las condiciones de humedad. Para maximizar el desempeno de las arcillas (y materiales similares), se sugiere realizar ensayos de Casagrande para determinar su indice de plasticidad. De este modo, podra seleccionarse preferentemente para los taludes expuestos el material con menor plasticidad (idealmente indice de plasticidad por debajo de 20), reservando los suelos de mayor plasticidad para zonas de bermas donde sea posible aplicar una compactacion controlada.^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: The recommendation to reserve clay use for situations requiring impermeability is sound given clay's demonstrated marginal stability performance on slopes. Clay properties do vary significantly with moisture and time due to swelling, softening, and weathering effects, particularly for high-plasticity clays [WEB:https://www.soilconnect.com/blog/understanding-plasticity-index-what-it-means-for-soil-performance]. The Casagrande plasticity chart is the standard method for determining plasticity index and classifying fine-grained soils [WEB:https://www.nzgs.org/libraries/nzgs20_hind/][WEB:https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atterberg_limits]. The recommendation to preferentially use lower plasticity materials (PI $< 20$) on slopes is consistent with geotechnical practice: soils with PI $> 20$-25 are generally more problematic due to higher shrink-swell potential and sensitivity to moisture [WEB:https://www.soilconnect.com/blog/the-atterberg-limits-explained-ll-pl-and-pi]. Lower plasticity soils are more predictable, easier to compact, and provide more reliable slope performance. Reserving high-plasticity materials for berms with controlled compaction is a practical strategy for utilizing available materials while managing stability risk. This recommendation is technically sound and consistent with the wide plasticity range (PI 13-96) documented for available clays.]

Finalmente, se recomienda contar con una caracterizacion de los volumenes de cada uno de los materiales de cobertura, lo cual permitira una planificacion de la ejecucion y una optimizacion de recursos en funcion de la seguridad requerida. Considerando que estos taludes piloto permitiran observar y medir el desempeno real de las alternativas, sumado a los factores de seguridad reducidos y la amplia variabilidad de los materiales empleados, se recomienda su ejecucion acompanada por un riguroso monitoreo en campo. Esto permitira recolectar informacion clave para futuras etapas de diseno y realizar ajustes en caso de desviaciones respecto a los objetivos previstos.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The recommendations for material volume characterization, execution planning, and rigorous field monitoring are entirely appropriate given the study context. Volume characterization enables optimization of material placement to match stability requirements (e.g., placing higher-performing materials on steeper slopes). The acknowledgment that these are pilot test slopes intended to measure real-world performance justifies the acceptance of configurations with marginal stability ($\mathrm{FoS} < 1.3$, $\mathrm{PoF}$ 5-10%) that might not be acceptable for production closure works. The wide material variability documented throughout the report (particularly for clays and organic soils) makes field monitoring essential to verify that actual performance matches design assumptions. This adaptive management approach—using instrumented test panels to inform final design—is best practice for mine closure cover design under uncertainty [WEB:https://www.geosystemsanalysis.com/mine_reclamation.html]. The recommendation to collect data for future design stages and make adjustments if deviations occur recognizes the iterative nature of closure design and the value of performance-based validation.]
