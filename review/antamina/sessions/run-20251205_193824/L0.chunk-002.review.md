El proyecto piloto de coberturas se ubica dentro del Botadero Este de la mina Antamina. La Figura 2-1 presenta el diseno para el reperfilado del botadero y la disposicion de los paneles de cobertura. El area de estudio comprende cinco taludes principales con una altura aproximada de 80 metros y pendientes variables entre 1.7V:1.0H y 2.2V:1.0H.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Project location and geometric specifications are site-specific design parameters that do not require external validation. The slope geometries (1.7:1 to 2.2:1) fall within typical ranges for waste rock dump closure designs [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379954492_Risk_Based_Design_Targets_for_Closure_of_Waste_Rock_Dumps].]

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The recommendation for intermediate benches is consistent with standard geotechnical practice for waste rock dump stability and safety during construction. #### Suelo organico

El suelo organico disponible proviene de acopios existentes en el sitio y presenta una granulometria variable, con contenido de finos entre 22% y 91%, y un contenido de gravas que varia entre 0 y 35%. Su plasticidad es moderada a alta, con indices de plasticidad entre 19 y 26 y limites liquidos de 56% a 63%.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The characterization describes highly variable organic soil with wide ranges in gradation and plasticity. The reported plasticity index ($\mathrm{PI}$ = 19-26) and liquid limit ($\mathrm{LL}$ = 56-63%) values are consistent with silty clays to clays of moderate to high plasticity (CL to CH classification per USCS). This variability is typical of stockpiled topsoil materials from different source areas.]

El estudio preliminar de alternativas de cierre [@GeoSystems2024] indica la presencia de dos tipos de materiales: un suelo organico fino, con un angulo de friccion interna de 25 grados y cohesion de 1 kPa, y otro de naturaleza mas gruesa, con un angulo de friccion de 30 grados y cohesion de 0.5 kPa. Los pesos unitarios adoptados son de 16.5 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ y 17 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$, respectivamente. Estos valores de peso unitario y cohesion se encuentran dentro del rango esperado [@JCSS2006]. Asimismo, de acuerdo con [@Sorensen2013], los angulos de friccion utilizados se encuentran dentro del rango esperable para suelos con los indices de plasticidad reportados, estimandose entre 23 y 30 grados, para indices de 26 y 19 respectivamente.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The organic soil parameters can be validated against published correlations. Sorensen and Okkels (2013) established correlations between drained peak friction angle and plasticity index for overconsolidated clays, showing friction angles decrease with increasing plasticity [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/285583666_Correlation_between_drained_shear_strength_and_plasticity_index_of_undisturbed_overconsolidated_clays]. For $\mathrm{PI}$ = 19-26, the cited range of $\phi' = 23°-30°$ aligns well with published data. Unit weights of 16.5-17 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ are typical for compacted silty clay to clay materials. Cohesion values of 0.5-1 kPa are low but reasonable for remolded/recompacted materials. The JCSS probabilistic model code provides statistical distributions for soil properties that support these ranges [WEB:https://www.jcss-lc.org/publications/jcsspmc/soilproperties_2006.pdf].]

#### Arcillas

Las arcillas propuestas como material de cubierta presentan una alta variabilidad mineralogica, mostrando limites liquidos entre 33 y 125 y un indice de plasticidad entre 13 y 96, lo que indica la presencia de minerales de alta plasticidad (como montmorillonita). Para su utilizacion, en el estudio preliminar [@GeoSystems2024] se plantea la compactacion al 95% de densidad maxima segun el ensayo Proctor estandar. Sin embargo, estos suelos exhiben comportamientos muy diversos segun su plasticidad: los suelos con indice de plasticidad menor a 15 o 20 probablemente puedan compactarse adecuadamente, pero los materiales con plasticidad mayor son incompatibles con ese grado de compactacion y, de ser necesario su empleo en zonas inclinadas, deberan ser estabilizados, tal como se senala en las recomendaciones. Dado que la estabilizacion puede ser antieconomica, se sugiere reservar los materiales de mayor plasticidad para bermas y sectores horizontales donde los requerimientos de compacidad y resistencia sean menores.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The extremely wide range of plasticity ($\mathrm{PI}$ = 13-96, $\mathrm{LL}$ = 33-125) indicates highly variable clay mineralogy spanning low-plasticity silts (ML/CL) to extremely high-plasticity clays (CH). The inference of montmorillonite presence based on high plasticity is correct. The technical concern regarding compaction difficulty for high-plasticity clays ($\mathrm{PI} > 20$) is well-founded: such materials are difficult to compact, prone to swelling/shrinkage, and exhibit significant strength loss upon wetting. The recommendation to restrict high-plasticity materials to benches and flat areas is sound engineering practice. Standard Proctor at 95% compaction may be unachievable for clays with $\mathrm{PI} > 30$ without modification.]

Para las condiciones de estabilidad pico se utilizan parametros representativos de una arcilla compactada: angulo de friccion de 22 grados y cohesion de 5 kPa; para grandes deformaciones, los valores son de 22 grados y 2 kPa, respectivamente.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The peak parameters ($\phi' = 22°$, $c' = 5$ kPa) and residual/large displacement parameters ($\phi' = 22°$, $c' = 2$ kPa) show cohesion degradation but constant friction angle. This is somewhat unusual; typically residual friction angles decrease by 2-4° from peak values for clays. The constant $\phi' = 22°$ suggests a friction angle closer to critical state. Cohesion reduction from 5 to 2 kPa reflects loss of apparent cohesion with progressive failure, which is realistic. However, maintaining identical friction angles between peak and residual states requires justification.]

Se reconoce que estos valores pueden variar en funcion del tiempo debido a procesos de hinchamiento o ablandamiento. El peso unitario considerado es 17 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$. Estos valores de peso unitario y cohesion se encuentran dentro del rango esperado [@JCSS2006]. Asimismo, de acuerdo con [@Sorensen2013], los angulos de friccion adoptados se encuentran dentro del rango esperable para arcillas con los indices de plasticidad reportados, estimandose angulos de friccion entre 17 y 27 grados, para indices entre 96 y 13 respectivamente.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The acknowledgment of time-dependent strength degradation through swelling or softening is critical for high-plasticity clays, particularly for long-term closure performance. Unit weight of 17 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ is typical for compacted clay. The cited Sorensen (2013) correlation supports $\phi' = 17°-27°$ for $\mathrm{PI} = 13-96$, and the adopted $\phi' = 22°$ falls centrally within this range. However, using a single friction angle for materials with such extreme plasticity variation (13 to 96) introduces significant uncertainty. Ideally, distinct parameter sets should be defined for low-plasticity ($\mathrm{PI} < 20$) versus high-plasticity ($\mathrm{PI} > 30$) clay materials.]

#### Arenas

La arena utilizada en las cubiertas corresponde a arena limpia colocada con una compactacion baja o nula, aunque una ligera compactacion adicional puede ocurrir tras la colocacion del suelo organico superior. Estas arenas aseguran una adecuada permeabilidad y son tambien utilizadas como material de cama bajo geomembranas. El material se caracteriza como no cohesivo, tipico de arenas para drenaje, y su resistencia se basa unicamente en la friccion interna. El estudio preliminar de alternativas de cierre [@GeoSystems2024] considera un angulo de friccion de 34 grados, cohesion nula, peso unitario humedo de 18.5 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ y saturado de 20.8 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$. Sin embargo, SRK considera que este angulo de friccion puede resultar elevado para un material colocado sin un control de compactacion y recomienda adoptar un valor de 31 grados, aproximadamente igual al angulo de friccion interna a volumen constante [@EPRI1990].

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The critique of the 34° friction angle for loose to uncompacted sand is technically sound. For clean sands without compaction control, friction angles typically range from 28-32° depending on relative density. The reduction to 31° based on constant volume friction angle ($\phi_{cv}$) is appropriate. The EPRI 1990 manual (Kulhawy and Mayne) provides widely accepted correlations for sand friction angles, with $\phi_{cv}$ representing the critical state friction angle typically 28-33° for clean sands [WEB:https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/research/infrastructure/structures/bridge/15034/002.cfm]. The saturated unit weight of 20.8 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ corresponds to void ratio $e \approx 0.6-0.7$, consistent with medium dense sand. The moist unit weight of 18.5 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ implies degree of saturation $S_r \approx 30-40\%$.]

#### Trisoplast

El Trisoplast es un material compuesto por arena, bentonita y un polimero, disenado para tener baja permeabilidad y cierta resistencia estructural. La interaccion del polimero con la bentonita y la arena genera una estructura tipo gel, aportando cohesion al ensamblado granular. El material es compactado al 95% de su densidad relativa y se emplea como barrera en la cubierta. Los parametros resistentes del material han sido provistos por el proveedor y han sido verificados mediante ensayos de laboratorio en otros estudios de analisis de estabilidad publicados [@Behrens2008].

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Trisoplast is a proprietary polymer-enhanced bentonite material used for low-permeability barriers in landfill and mine closure applications. The description of gel structure formation from polymer-bentonite-sand interaction is accurate. However, the specific citation [@Behrens2008] could not be verified through external sources. Alternative published research on Trisoplast includes studies by Schanz, Agus, and Tscheschlok on hydro-mechanical properties [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/288305147_Schanz_Agus_and_Tscheschlok_Hydromechanical_properties_of_Trisoplast_for_landfill_applications] and work by Weitz and Boels on application for landfill lining. The claim of 95% compaction to relative density should specify whether this refers to modified Proctor or another standard. Reliance on manufacturer-provided parameters warrants independent verification testing.]

Para el analisis se consideran un angulo de friccion interna de 30 grados y una cohesion de 10 kPa, junto a un peso unitario de 16.6 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$, valores que reflejan su comportamiento de tipo "arena cementada" con cohesion aportada por su estructura interna.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The parameters ($\phi' = 30°$, $c' = 10$ kPa, $\gamma = 16.6$ $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$) are consistent with the "cemented sand" behavior description. Published research indicates Trisoplast exhibits high friction angles combined with significant cohesion from polymer-bentonite bonding [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/270427783_Design_parameters_for_bentonite-enhanced_sand_as_a_landfill_liner]. The cohesion of 10 kPa is substantial and derives from the polymer-bentonite gel structure rather than true cohesion. Unit weight of 16.6 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ is plausible for a sand-bentonite mixture with approximately 5-15% bentonite content. However, these parameters should be verified through project-specific testing, as Trisoplast properties can vary with sand type, bentonite content, polymer dosage, and curing conditions. The absence of residual parameters is a limitation for stability analyses involving large deformations.]

#### Mezcla de relaves y roca triturada (commingled tailings)

Las mezclas de relaves y roca triturada corresponden a combinaciones de materiales con granulometria predominantemente gruesa, usando proporciones de entre 4:1 y 1:1 (roca de desmonte:relave), y considerando relaves filtrados, en pasta y ciclonados. Los parametros resistentes del material han sido determinados por [@Golder2021] a traves de ensayos triaxiales, los cuales muestran que bajo 100 kPa de confinamiento el material se comporta como un material granular puro en las condiciones del sistema de cubierta, con cohesion despreciable y angulos de friccion internos entre 41.5 y 43 grados. El peso unitario estimado es de 19.0 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$, y la capacidad de resistencia proviene de la friccion entre particulas de roca y relave.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Commingled waste rock and tailings mixtures have been extensively studied for dry-stack tailings applications. Published research confirms that waste rock-tailings mixtures at low confining pressures (<100 kPa) exhibit friction angles of 38-43° depending on mixture ratio, with waste rock showing $\phi' = 41°$ and co-mixed specimens averaging $\phi' = 38-40°$ [WEB:https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11440-015-0429-1]. The reported range of 41.5-43° is at the upper end but consistent with 4:1 ratios favoring waste rock behavior. Triaxial testing is the appropriate method for such materials [WEB:https://www.mdpi.com/2075-163X/13/2/295]. However, the specific [@Golder2021] reference could not be independently verified. The negligible cohesion and unit weight of 19.0 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ are reasonable. For 1:1 mixtures with higher tailings content, friction angles would be expected toward the lower end of the range.]

Para el analisis se considera un angulo de friccion con un valor medio de 42 grados. Si bien este valor medio puede considerarse algo elevado para este tipo de suelos, especialmente en las mezclas con proporciones 1:1, en el analisis de sensibilidad se evaluaron tambien casos con un angulo de 37.8 grados, valor que resulta mas adecuado para dichas condiciones.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The acknowledgment that $\phi' = 42°$ may be high for 1:1 mixtures shows appropriate technical judgment. The sensitivity analysis using $\phi' = 37.8°$ (representing approximately 10% reduction from 42°) provides a more conservative bound that aligns better with published data for tailings-rich commingled materials. Research shows effective friction angles for co-mixed specimens range from 38-40° with tailings content significantly influencing the result [WEB:https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11440-015-0429-1]. The 37.8° value is well-supported for 1:1 mixtures. This two-value approach (42° base case, 37.8° sensitivity) brackets the uncertainty appropriately.]

#### Relleno de desmonte (waste rock)

El relleno de desmonte esta conformado por material de roca ocupando un rango amplio de granulometria, presentando una envolvente de resistencia no lineal y dependencia del nivel de confinamiento. Para los analisis de estabilidad a baja profundidad, se adopta una funcion de resistencia no lineal definida por Antamina, sin presencia de cohesion, respaldado por ensayos previos y observaciones de campo [@Piteau2022]. Su peso unitario es de 19.6 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The description of waste rock behavior is technically accurate. Waste rock exhibits strongly non-linear shear strength envelopes with stress-dependent friction angles due to particle breakage at higher confining stresses. At shallow depths (low confinement) typical of cover systems, friction angles are higher than at depth. Zero cohesion is appropriate for uncemented waste rock. The use of site-specific nonlinear strength functions from large-scale testing [@Piteau2022] is best practice for waste rock characterization [WEB:https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/17480930.2024.2367977]. Unit weight of 19.6 $\mathrm{kN/m}^3$ is typical for dumped waste rock with void ratio $e \approx 0.35-0.45$, consistent with porosity around 25-30%.]

#### Interfaz geomembrana y arena

La interaccion entre la geomembrana texturizada de HDPE/LLDPE y la arena drenante determina la resistencia por friccion y adhesion en la interfaz, influyendo en la estabilidad ante deslizamientos superficiales. El angulo de friccion interna de la interfaz es 31 grados y la adhesion es 1 kPa en condiciones de esfuerzo pico; en condiciones de grandes deformaciones, el angulo de friccion disminuye a 26 grados, manteniendose la adhesion en 1 kPa. Estos valores se han determinado mediante ensayos especificos y representan un comportamiento intermedio entre el material granular puro y la interfaz de materiales sinteticos [@Howell2016]. Para el diseno ejecutivo, estos valores asumidos deberan ser validados o ajustados de acuerdo con las especificaciones del proveedor.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The geomembrane-sand interface parameters are within published ranges but the citation [@Howell2016] could not be verified. Extensive research on HDPE geomembrane-sand interfaces shows peak friction angles typically 65-90% of sand friction angle, and for sand with $\phi' = 31°$, interface values of 26-31° are reasonable [WEB:https://www.layfieldgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Geomembrane-Interface-Friction.pdf]. The peak interface friction angle of 31° represents approximately 100% of sand friction angle, which is high and more typical of textured geomembranes with angular sand. Smooth HDPE typically gives $\delta = 0.5-0.7 \phi_{sand}$ or 18-25° [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Interface-friction-angles_tbl1_237378082]. The residual value of 26° and the 5° degradation from peak to residual is consistent with observed behavior. Adhesion (interface cohesion) of 1 kPa at both peak and residual is unusual and requires verification. The recommendation for project-specific interface testing is essential given the critical role of this interface in veneer stability.]

### Criterios de diseno

Para la evaluacion de la estabilidad se siguieron los lineamientos adoptados por [@Piteau2022] y [@Stantec2024], basados en los criterios definidos en [@Golder2016]. Los factores de seguridad ($\mathrm{FoS}$) minimos recomendados se indican en la siguiente tabla:

| Condicion | $\mathrm{FoS}_{\mathrm{min}}$ |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Estabilidad superficial de coberturas. Condicion estatica | 1.30 |
| Estabilidad global de taludes. Condicion estatica | 1.50 |
| Condicion de carga extrema (sismo). Pseudo-estatico | 1.00 |

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The factor of safety criteria ($\mathrm{FoS} = 1.30$ for cover stability, $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.50$ for global stability, $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.00$ for seismic pseudo-static) are consistent with industry guidelines for mine closure. Published standards indicate $\mathrm{FoS} > 1.5$ for good design reliability in static conditions and $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.15-1.30$ for acceptable designs depending on consequence level [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379954492_Risk_Based_Design_Targets_for_Closure_of_Waste_Rock_Dumps]. The lower criterion of 1.30 for cover stability versus 1.50 for global stability reflects the lower consequence of shallow cover failure versus deep-seated slope failure. For seismic pseudo-static analysis, $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.00$ represents the yield threshold where permanent deformation begins; values $> 1.0$ imply no movement while values $< 1.0$ imply permanent displacement [WEB:https://www.researchgate.net/publication/315474880_Performance-Based_Slope_Stability_Analysis_and_the_Pseudo-Static_Factor_of_Safety]. However, modern practice often requires $\mathrm{FoS} = 1.1-1.3$ for seismic conditions depending on allowable deformation.]

Ademas de los factores de seguridad considerados en el analisis deterministico, en este estudio se ha tomado en cuenta la probabilidad de falla (PoF) como criterio complementario de aceptacion del diseno. De acuerdo con lo recomendado por [@Hawley2017], cuando los datos disponibles presentan baja confiabilidad y las consecuencias asociadas a una eventual falla son moderadas o bajas, resulta apropiado aceptar valores de PoF maxima de 10%, en correspondencia con factores de seguridad mayores a 1.3. La adopcion de este criterio permite una evaluacion mas realista de la seguridad, incorporando la incertidumbre inherente a los parametros empleados en el analisis de estabilidad.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The use of probability of failure (PoF) as a complementary criterion to deterministic $\mathrm{FoS}$ represents best practice in risk-informed geotechnical design. Hawley and Cunning's 2017 book "Guidelines for Mine Waste Dump and Stockpile Design" provides comprehensive guidance on design acceptance criteria incorporating both deterministic and probabilistic approaches [WEB:https://www.routledge.com/Guidelines-for-Mine-Waste-Dump-and-Stockpile-Design/Hawley-Cunning/p/book/9781138197312]. However, the specific claim that 10% PoF is "appropriate" for low-moderate consequences and low data reliability requires careful interpretation. While 10% PoF is cited, current risk-based frameworks emphasize ALARP (As Low As Reasonably Practicable) principles rather than fixed thresholds. The correspondence between $\mathrm{FoS} > 1.3$ and $\mathrm{PoF} < 10\%$ assumes typical CoV values and is approximate. For closure applications, stakeholder acceptance of residual risk levels is project-specific [WEB:https://papers.acg.uwa.edu.au/p/2215_14_Carter/]. The 10% PoF threshold should be validated against site-specific consequence assessment and owner risk tolerance.]

Segun lo establecido por [@Piteau2022] en el diseno preliminar, las consecuencias asociadas a la falla de la cobertura se consideran bajas y la confianza en las propiedades de los materiales es baja a moderada. Esta apreciacion se refuerza por el hecho de que la reparacion de posibles danos en la cobertura puede ser realizada con relativa facilidad. Este enfoque esta en linea con los criterios de [@Hawley2017], que clasifican como moderada la consecuencia de falla en botaderos con pendientes globales menores a 30 grados y alturas inferiores a 250 m, o con pendientes iguales al angulo de reposo de menos de 100 m de altura, siempre que no exista infraestructura critica aguas abajo, acceso irrestricto o bien existan medidas robustas de mitigacion y contencion para proteger elementos sensibles ante un evento de inestabilidad.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: The consequence classification as "low" for cover failure is reasonable given the shallow nature of potential failures and reparability. The referenced geometric criteria from Hawley (2017) for moderate consequence classification (slopes $< 30°$, heights $< 250$ m for gentle slopes or $< 100$ m for angles of repose) provide quantitative thresholds. The Antamina waste rock dump geometry (slopes 1.7:1 to 2.2:1 equivalent to 30-36°, heights ~80 m) falls near the moderate consequence boundary. The factors reducing consequence include: (1) no critical infrastructure downstream, (2) controlled access, and (3) shallow depth of cover failures limiting runout. However, classification of consequences should also consider environmental impacts (acid rock drainage, sediment release), regulatory implications, and reputational risk beyond physical damage alone. The final consequence level should be determined jointly with the mine operator considering corporate risk tolerance.]

La definicion final del nivel de consecuencia y, por ende, la probabilidad de falla admisible debe ser determinada y asumida en conjunto con Compania Minera Antamina de acuerdo con sus estandares internos, su nivel de tolerancia al riesgo y los objetivos de manejo ambiental y operacional del sitio.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: This statement appropriately recognizes that consequence classification and acceptable PoF thresholds are ultimately owner decisions that must align with corporate risk tolerance, regulatory obligations, and stakeholder commitments. Modern mine closure frameworks emphasize that design acceptance criteria should be established collaboratively between technical consultants and mine operators, with consideration of post-mining land use objectives and long-term stewardship responsibilities. The ALARP principle requires that stakeholders agree the planned design reduces residual risk to levels as low as reasonably possible for site-specific circumstances [WEB:https://papers.acg.uwa.edu.au/p/2215_14_Carter/].]

### Metodos de analisis

#### Estabilidad superficial de cobertura

La estabilidad de las diferentes alternativas para el sistema de cubierta fue evaluada utilizando metodos de estabilidad de taludes por equilibrio limite. En este tipo de analisis, se compara la resistencia de los materiales del suelo frente a las fuerzas gravitacionales que pueden provocar inestabilidad en el talud.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Limit equilibrium methods are the standard approach for slope stability analysis and are well-established for cover system evaluation. The fundamental principle of comparing available shear resistance to gravitational driving forces through factor of safety calculations is universally accepted in geotechnical engineering practice.]

Se utilizo el procedimiento del talud infinito para el analisis de la cobertura dado que, cuando la cobertura es de un espesor reducido en comparacion con la longitud de la pendiente, se puede suponer que el talud es "infinito", lo que implica que la resistencia que aporta el pie del talud es minima y se puede despreciar. Este metodo considera que cualquier posible deslizamiento ocurre de manera paralela a la cara de la pendiente, lo que simplifica considerablemente el analisis.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The infinite slope method is the appropriate and widely accepted approach for analyzing shallow cover system failures. The method applies when slope length greatly exceeds depth, allowing assumption of two-dimensional plane-strain conditions with failure parallel to the slope surface. For veneer cover soils on containment facilities, infinite slope analysis provides reliable results as the failure mechanism is predominantly translational [WEB:https://www.geoengineer.org/education/slope-stability/slope-stability-analysis/slope-stability-single-free-body-procedures]. The assumption that toe resistance is negligible is valid for thin covers on long slopes. The method is particularly relevant for geomembrane-soil interfaces and layered cover systems where the weakest plane controls stability.]

Esta metodologia es aplicable sin caer en supuestos conservadores siempre que la altura vertical del talud sea al menos 30 veces mayor que el espesor de la cobertura.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The 30:1 ratio criterion (slope height to cover thickness) for applicability of infinite slope method is a well-established guideline. Published sources indicate the method applies to slopes with length-to-depth ratio of at least 20:1 [WEB:http://civilwares.free.fr/Geotechnical engineering - Principles and Practices of Soils Mechanics and Foundation Engineering/Chapter 10.pdf]. For an 80 m high slope with 0.3-1.0 m thick cover, the ratio ranges from 80:1 to 27:1. Most sections exceed the 30:1 threshold, validating the infinite slope assumption. For the thickest covers (~1 m) on shortest slopes, the ratio approaches the threshold and results should be interpreted recognizing this boundary condition. Below 20:1, finite slope methods become necessary to account for toe and crest effects.]

Para llevar a cabo los calculos deterministicos, se utilizo una hoja de calculo en Excel, donde se detallaron los datos intermedios y resultados obtenidos. Ademas de considerar los valores medios de los parametros geotecnicos, se realizo un analisis de sensibilidad en el que se variaron de manera aislada la cohesion y el angulo de friccion interna segun los porcentajes indicados.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Use of spreadsheet-based calculations for infinite slope analysis is standard practice given the closed-form analytical solutions. Spreadsheets provide transparency in calculations and facilitate sensitivity analyses through parametric variation. One-at-a-time sensitivity analysis varying $\phi'$ and $c'$ independently identifies the relative influence of each parameter on stability. This complements the probabilistic analysis by showing parameter importance. The approach is documented in published guidance on practical reliability analysis using spreadsheets [WEB:https://cdnsciencepub.com/doi/10.1139/t10-044].]

De manera complementaria, se realizo una evaluacion probabilistica de la estabilidad de taludes, con el objetivo de incorporar explicitamente la incertidumbre asociada a la variabilidad de los parametros resistentes de los materiales de las coberturas.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Probabilistic slope stability analysis provides significant advantages over purely deterministic approaches by explicitly quantifying uncertainty in material parameters and calculating probability of failure. This approach is increasingly recognized as best practice for geotechnical design, particularly for projects with limited data quality or long-term performance requirements such as mine closure. Probabilistic methods enable risk-informed decision making and calculation of reliability indices.]

Se llevaron a cabo simulaciones de Monte Carlo con un total de 10,000 muestras. En cada simulacion se variaron aleatoriamente los valores del angulo de friccion interna y la cohesion de los diferentes suelos presentes, tomando como base la caracterizacion mecanica realizada. Los parametros de friccion y cohesion se definieron mediante distribuciones normales truncadas, utilizando como valores medios aquellos obtenidos de la caracterizacion mecanica de cada material. Los desvios estandar se fijaron como fraccion del valor medio: un 10% para el angulo de friccion interna y un 25% para la cohesion, en concordancia con lo considerado para el analisis de sensibilidad. Asimismo, se establecieron limites minimos y maximos para evitar la simulacion de valores no representativos del comportamiento de los diferentes tipos de suelo.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The Monte Carlo implementation details are technically sound. A sample size of 10,000 is robust and well-established for slope stability probabilistic analysis, providing adequate precision for estimating failure probabilities in the 1-10% range [WEB:https://www.mdpi.com/2075-1680/12/5/481] [WEB:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0266352X1000114X]. Use of truncated normal distributions prevents physically unrealistic parameter values (negative cohesion, friction angles outside feasible ranges). The coefficient of variation values (CoV = 10% for $\phi'$, 25% for $c'$) are consistent with JCSS recommendations for soils with limited data [WEB:https://www.jcss-lc.org/jcss-probabilistic-model-code/]. Treating parameters as uncorrelated random variables is a simplifying assumption; in reality, $\phi'$ and $c'$ often exhibit negative correlation, but this effect is typically minor for slope stability. The approach enables calculation of PoF for comparison against the 10% acceptance threshold.]

En particular, se hizo un analisis separado para dos tipos de suelo organico (topsoil), diferenciados segun su contenido de finos, a fin de evaluar el desempeno de cada uno en distintas secciones y configuraciones de coberturas.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Distinguishing between fine organic soil ($\phi' = 25°$) and coarse organic soil ($\phi' = 30°$) for separate probabilistic analyses is appropriate given the documented variability in fines content (22-91%) and the 5° difference in friction angle. This differentiation enables more realistic assessment of cover performance depending on which material is actually placed in each section. Given the high spatial variability of stockpiled topsoil, this approach provides bounds on expected performance rather than a single deterministic prediction.]

#### Estabilidad global del talud

Para el analisis de la estabilidad del talud de global, los factores de seguridad se determinaron mediante metodos de equilibrio limite utilizando el software especializado Slide2 de Rocscience. En particular, se analizaron posibles mecanismos de falla tanto circulares como poligonales, empleando los metodos de Spencer y Morgenstern-Price, que permiten evaluar de manera precisa la estabilidad ante deslizamientos complejos.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The use of Slide2 software with Spencer and Morgenstern-Price methods represents industry-standard practice for global slope stability analysis. Both methods are rigorous limit equilibrium approaches that satisfy complete force and moment equilibrium, making them more accurate than simplified methods like Ordinary/Fellenius or Bishop for non-circular failure surfaces [WEB:https://www.rocscience.com/help/slide2/documentation/slide-model/project-settings/methods/analysis-methods]. Spencer's method assumes constant interslice force inclination, while Morgenstern-Price uses a user-defined function; both typically yield very similar results and provide thrust line for analyzing tension cracks. The simultaneous analysis of circular and non-circular (polygonal) failure surfaces ensures the critical mechanism is identified, as waste rock dumps can fail along composite surfaces involving weak interfaces or benches.]

En la modelacion, se asumio que el extremo este del deposito de residuos (East WRF) esta fundado sobre materiales de roca base con mayor resistencia que la propia roca de desecho. Por este motivo, no se consideraron posibles superficies de deslizamiento profundas que atraviesen la fundacion, ya que se espera que el mayor potencial de inestabilidad se encuentre restringido a la masa de waste rock.

^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: The assumption of competent bedrock foundation stronger than waste rock is reasonable for many waste rock dump sites and simplifies the analysis by constraining failure surfaces to the waste rock mass. This assumption is common when bedrock is fresh, unweathered rock with high intact strength and limited discontinuity persistence. However, this assumption should be validated through: (1) subsurface investigation confirming bedrock quality, (2) review of potential weak zones or weathered horizons at the waste rock-bedrock interface, and (3) consideration of whether foundation drainage conditions (pore pressures in bedrock) could contribute to instability. The statement that "the greatest potential for instability is restricted to the waste rock mass" is generally valid but requires verification that no weak seams, shear zones, or adverse groundwater conditions exist in the foundation. For final design, sensitivity analyses testing foundation strength parameters would strengthen confidence in this assumption.]

Para este informe se selecciono una seccion representativa con la mayor pendiente proyectada (1.7H:1V) y una altura total de 50 metros, configurando el escenario mas critico para la evaluacion de la estabilidad.

^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Selection of the steepest slope (1.7H:1V = 30.6° overall angle) and a representative height of 50 m for detailed global stability analysis is appropriate for identifying the critical design case. The 1.7:1 slope is steeper than the other analyzed slopes (2:1, 2.2:1) and thus represents a conservative basis for stability assessment. The 50 m height is less than the stated 80 m height of individual slopes, suggesting the analysis focuses on a single lift or partial slope height. For comprehensive assessment, consideration should be given to analyzing: (1) the full 80 m height if multiple lifts stack vertically, (2) effects of varying groundwater conditions, and (3) implications of the 50 m analysis height for design acceptance if actual slopes reach 80 m. The identification of a "critical scenario" is standard practice for establishing design adequacy across varying geometric configurations.]
