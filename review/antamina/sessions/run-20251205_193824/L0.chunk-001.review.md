### Objetivo

El objetivo del presente estudio consiste en evaluar la estabilidad fisica de las coberturas segun las configuraciones propuestas bajo condiciones estaticas y dinamicas. El desempeno en condiciones reales de las coberturas en estos paneles de prueba sera evaluado para realizar los ajustes necesarios de cara al diseno definitivo de cierre.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The stated objective aligns with standard practice for mine closure cover system design. Best practices in mine reclamation require testing cover alternatives through pilot-scale test plots to evaluate performance under field conditions before final closure design [WEB:https://www.geosystemsanalysis.com/mine_reclamation.html]. Static and dynamic stability analysis is a fundamental requirement for waste rock dump closure designs, particularly in seismically active regions such as Peru [WEB:https://www.e3s-conferences.org/articles/e3sconf/abs/2024/99/e3sconf_geoamericas2024_16004/e3sconf_geoamericas2024_16004.html]. The iterative approach of testing trial panels before finalizing closure design is consistent with risk-based mine closure methodology.]

### Alcances

Este es un estudio de gabinete de ingenieria a nivel basico, basado en la informacion suministrada por GeoSystems y Antamina. El analisis y validacion de esta informacion esta fuera del alcance del servicio de SRK.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The scope statement defining a basic-level desktop engineering study based on client-supplied information is consistent with standard consulting practice. Engineering consultants routinely rely on client-provided data and explicitly define scope limitations regarding data validation [WEB:https://www.srk.com/en/services/mine-waste-management]. This scoping approach is typical for preliminary or conceptual-level studies where the consultant's role is analysis rather than data collection or verification.]

El alcance del servicio se limita a proporcionar recomendaciones para la caracterizacion de los materiales de cobertura, revisar la amenaza sismica y realizar un analisis de desempeno de las alternativas de cobertura frente a escenarios estaticos y dinamicos. La estabilidad del botadero de base se analiza en forma aislada de manera de cuantificar la seguridad del sistema de soporte.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The defined scope elements are standard components of cover system stability analysis. Material characterization, seismic hazard assessment, and static/dynamic performance analysis are fundamental requirements for geotechnical design of mine closure covers [WEB:https://www.srk.com/en/publications/waste-rock-dump-management-and-stability-evaluation]. Analyzing the underlying waste dump stability independently from the cover system is appropriate methodology to quantify the foundation support capacity and establish baseline stability conditions [WEB:https://www.srk.com/en/projects/cerro-vanguardia-gold-mine].]

El efecto de la erosion de la cobertura y el impacto de deformaciones diferidas en el botadero y su erosion quedan excluidos de este analisis.^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The explicit exclusion of erosion effects and delayed deformations represents a scope limitation that may affect the comprehensiveness of the stability analysis. While static and dynamic stability analyses are commonly performed as isolated assessments, long-term performance of closure covers is affected by erosion and time-dependent deformations [WEB:https://www.geosystemsanalysis.com/mine_reclamation.html]. This exclusion is acceptable for a basic-level study but would typically need to be addressed in detailed design phases. The statement accurately discloses these limitations.]
