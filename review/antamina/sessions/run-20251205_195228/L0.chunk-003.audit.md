### Estabilidad global de taludes

Como aproximacion, la verificacion de estabilidad global del talud se verifico para la pendiente maxima de 1.7:1 y una altura de 50 m considerando un unico material de relleno (waste rock). Mediante los metodos de equilibrio limite de Morgenstern-Price y Spencer se analizaron fallas circulares y poligonales y se determino un coeficiente de seguridad minimo de 1.60, el cual satisface los valores indicados previamente.^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Session 1 (HIGH confidence) considers the analysis approach technically sound, noting that evaluating global stability for the steepest slope (1.7:1) and maximum height (50 m) represents the most critical condition appropriate for preliminary assessment. Session 1 confirms Morgenstern-Price and Spencer methods are rigorous limit equilibrium techniques that are industry-standard, and that analyzing both circular and polygonal failure surfaces is prudent. The calculated minimum FoS = 1.60 exceeds the 1.50 criterion for global slope stability. Session 2 (MEDIUM confidence) agrees the result comfortably exceeds the criterion and that the methods are appropriate, but identifies several limiting aspects: (1) analysis is described as "approximation" using simplified geometry, (2) model assumes homogeneous waste rock without accounting for cover layers or weak zones, (3) critical foundation assumption (competent bedrock) cannot be independently verified, (4) no seismic analysis results presented despite being in stated scope, and (5) waste rock strength parameters rely on unverified site-specific nonlinear function from Piteau2022. Session 2 notes FoS = 1.60 is plausible for well-drained waste rock on competent foundation, but simplified analysis approach means actual global stability under all conditions may differ. Consensus confidence is MEDIUM due to Session 2's identification of multiple limiting factors and lack of verification of key assumptions.]
