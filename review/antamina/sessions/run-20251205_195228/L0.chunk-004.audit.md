## Recomendaciones

Las recomendaciones presentadas a continuacion se fundamentan tanto en los resultados del analisis deterministico y de sensibilidad como en los hallazgos derivados de la evaluacion probabilistica de estabilidad.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Both sessions agree this statement accurately describes the integration of multiple analytical approaches (deterministic, sensitivity, and probabilistic analyses) as the basis for recommendations. This multi-method approach is consistent with modern geotechnical design practice for risk-informed decision making and provides a more comprehensive understanding of stability and uncertainty than any single method alone.]

El analisis desarrollado sobre las alternativas de coberturas y sus respectivas condiciones de estabilidad ha permitido identificar que la mayoria de las alternativas evaluadas cumplen adecuadamente con los criterios minimos de seguridad deterministicos, salvo en ciertos casos donde la configuracion de material y pendiente resulta mas exigente: el suelo organico en taludes mas inclinados y los suelos finos en pendientes intermedias.^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: Both sessions confirm this summary accurately reflects the verification results showing most configurations met FoS >= 1.3, with exceptions for organic topsoil on 1.7:1 slopes (mean FoS = 1.17) and clay on 2.2:1 slopes (mean FoS = 1.18). Session 1 rates this HIGH confidence noting the characterization of these as "more demanding" is appropriate. Session 2 rates MEDIUM, noting that "most alternatives adequately meet" may be optimistic given parameter uncertainties and marginal compliance in some cases. Consensus is MEDIUM-HIGH given accurate reflection of results but acknowledgment of interpretive nuance regarding adequacy.]

A partir de ello, se profundizo el estudio incorporando un enfoque probabilistico, que permitio evaluar la influencia de la variabilidad de los parametros geotecnicos. El analisis probabilistico permitio estimar la probabilidad de falla (PoF) de cada alternativa, aportando una vision mas realista del desempeno esperado bajo condiciones de incertidumbre.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Both sessions agree this statement correctly describes the methodological progression from deterministic to probabilistic analysis. The probabilistic approach (Monte Carlo simulation with parameter distributions) explicitly accounts for geotechnical parameter uncertainty and provides probability of failure estimates. This approach is recognized in geotechnical reliability literature as providing a more realistic assessment of performance under uncertainty compared to single-value deterministic analysis.]

En la mayoria de las configuraciones evaluadas, la PoF resulto menor al 1%, confirmando un nivel de seguridad elevado. Las unicas excepciones se observaron en coincidencia con los casos en donde el analisis deterministico determino FoS menores a los requeridos. En estos casos, la PoF se encuentra entre el 5 y el 10%. Estas probabilidades pueden considerarse aceptables dentro del criterio de diseno adoptado para consecuencias de falla moderadas, conforme a lo recomendado por guias internacionales [@Hawley2017]. La definicion final del nivel de consecuencia y, por ende, la probabilidad de falla admisible debe ser determinada y asumida en conjunto con Compania Minera Antamina de acuerdo con sus estandares internos, su nivel de tolerancia al riesgo y los objetivos de manejo ambiental y operacional del sitio.^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Both sessions agree the results summary is accurate (six of ten configurations showed PoF < 1%, with elevated PoF of 5-10% where mean FoS < 1.3) and that the recommendation to jointly determine acceptability with the client is appropriate. Both note that 5-10% PoF values are relatively high compared to typical practice for permanent engineered structures, though may be acceptable for test panels with monitoring capability. The specific acceptability depends on consequence classification validation and stakeholder risk tolerance. Both sessions acknowledge the reference to Hawley 2017 for moderate consequence criteria, though note this cannot be independently verified and depends critically on data confidence and consequence classification validity.]

En funcion de los resultados integrados de los distintos enfoques de analisis, se establece el siguiente ranking de desempeno para las alternativas de cobertura, ordenado segun los criterios de estabilidad superficial alcanzados en los distintos escenarios evaluados:^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Both sessions agree that establishing a performance ranking based on integrated results from multiple analysis approaches is a logical synthesis providing practical guidance for design optimization and material selection based on stability performance.]

1. Coberturas multicapas con materiales compuestos (arenas limpias, mezclas de relaves, desmonte, geomembranas y Trisoplast): muestran el mayor margen de seguridad y respuesta estable ante solicitaciones estaticas.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Both sessions confirm the results table supports this ranking. Multi-layer composite systems showed the highest mean FoS values: Alternative J with commingled tailings (mean FoS = 1.51), Alternative D with geomembrane (mean FoS = 1.37), and Alternative I with Trisoplast (mean FoS = 1.32). All achieved mean FoS > 1.3 and very low PoF (< 0.4%), confirming high safety margins and stable response. The superior performance is attributable to higher strength parameters of these materials compared to organic topsoil and clay.]

2. Coberturas con arenas: mantienen valores de seguridad satisfactorios y presentan baja sensibilidad ante variaciones en la geometria del talud.^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Both sessions note that sand layers were components of Alternatives B, D, I, and J as drainage layers, and that independent evidence for this specific ranking position is limited by the absence of sand-only alternatives in the test program. Session 1 notes the statement appears based on general behavior of sand materials (non-cohesive, angle-of-friction-dependent stability) and their role in successful multi-layer systems. Session 2 similarly observes that sand drainage layers contributed to higher stability in multi-layer systems. Both acknowledge the statement is partially supported but lacks complete independent validation from the results table.]

3. Coberturas con suelos finos o arcillas: el desempeno es adecuado solo cuando se emplean materiales seleccionados y pendientes moderadas.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Both sessions confirm Alternative B (clay) demonstrates the conditional nature of clay cover performance. On 2.2:1 slopes, clay showed mean FoS = 1.18 with PoF = 5.13%, falling below the FoS >= 1.3 criterion. This confirms adequate performance requires "selected materials and moderate slopes." Clay materials exhibit high variability in properties depending on plasticity, mineralogy, and compaction. The wide plasticity range (PI 13-96) noted for available clays supports the need for material selection. The ranking reflects inherent challenges of using fine-grained cohesive soils on slopes.]

4. Coberturas con suelo organico en pendientes pronunciadas: muestran los menores factores de seguridad y probabilidades de falla mas altas, requiriendo optimizacion en su diseno y control en su ejecucion. El nivel de riesgo se encuadra dentro de los margenes definidos por las guias y criterios adoptados, pero debe confirmarse segun la gestion de riesgo interna.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Both sessions confirm Alternative A (organic topsoil) on 1.7:1 slopes consistently showed the lowest mean FoS = 1.17 and highest PoF = 8.10% across multiple sections. This unambiguously confirms organic soil on steep slopes exhibits the lowest factors of safety and highest failure probabilities. The need for design optimization and execution control is justified given marginal stability (FoS < 1.3). The acknowledgment that risk levels fall within adopted guideline margins (referencing the 10% PoF threshold) while requiring internal risk management confirmation is appropriate. The relatively low strength parameters of organic soil (friction angle 25-30 degrees, cohesion 0.5-1 kPa) combined with steep 1.7:1 slope geometry explain the poor performance ranking.]

En el caso particular de la arcilla, se recomienda su uso primordialmente en aquellas situaciones donde las condiciones de impermeabilidad lo demanden expresamente, dado que sus propiedades pueden variar considerablemente con el tiempo y las condiciones de humedad. Para maximizar el desempeno de las arcillas (y materiales similares), se sugiere realizar ensayos de Casagrande para determinar su indice de plasticidad. De este modo, podra seleccionarse preferentemente para los taludes expuestos el material con menor plasticidad (idealmente indice de plasticidad por debajo de 20), reservando los suelos de mayor plasticidad para zonas de bermas donde sea posible aplicar una compactacion controlada.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Both sessions agree this recommendation is sound engineering practice. Reserving clay use for situations requiring impermeability is appropriate given demonstrated marginal stability performance on slopes. Clay properties vary significantly with moisture and time due to swelling, softening, and weathering effects, particularly for high-plasticity clays. The Casagrande plasticity chart is the standard method for determining plasticity index and classifying fine-grained soils. The recommendation to preferentially use lower plasticity materials (PI < 20) on slopes is consistent with geotechnical practice: soils with PI > 20-25 are generally more problematic due to higher shrink-swell potential and sensitivity to moisture. Reserving high-plasticity materials for berms with controlled compaction is a practical strategy for utilizing available materials while managing stability risk. This recommendation addresses the wide plasticity range (PI 13-96) documented for available clays.]

Finalmente, se recomienda contar con una caracterizacion de los volumenes de cada uno de los materiales de cobertura, lo cual permitira una planificacion de la ejecucion y una optimizacion de recursos en funcion de la seguridad requerida. Considerando que estos taludes piloto permitiran observar y medir el desempeno real de las alternativas, sumado a los factores de seguridad reducidos y la amplia variabilidad de los materiales empleados, se recomienda su ejecucion acompanada por un riguroso monitoreo en campo. Esto permitira recolectar informacion clave para futuras etapas de diseno y realizar ajustes en caso de desviaciones respecto a los objetivos previstos.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: Both sessions agree the recommendations for material volume characterization, execution planning, and rigorous field monitoring are entirely appropriate and essential best practices for test panel programs. Volume characterization enables optimization of material placement to match stability requirements. The acknowledgment that these are pilot test slopes intended to measure real-world performance justifies acceptance of configurations with marginal stability (FoS < 1.3, PoF 5-10%) that might not be acceptable for production closure works. Given acknowledged study limitations (low parameter confidence, rejected site-specific test results, lack of independent validation, marginal stability results, and high material variability documented throughout the report), field monitoring is critical to validate design assumptions and allow for corrective action if performance deviates from predictions. This adaptive management approach using instrumented test panels to inform final design is best practice for mine closure cover design under uncertainty. The recommendation explicitly recognizes that reduced factors of safety and wide material variability necessitate rigorous monitoring, demonstrating appropriate engineering judgment.]
