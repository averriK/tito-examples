## Introduccion

GeoSystems Analysis (GeoSystems) lleva a cabo estudios de cierre para el Botadero Este de la Compania Minera Antamina S.A. (Antamina). SRK Consulting (Argentina) S.A. analiza la estabilidad de los paneles de prueba que componen las alternativas de coberturas a emplazar sobre el Botadero Este.^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: External evidence confirms GeoSystems Analysis conducts mine closure studies in Peru and SRK Consulting Argentina provides waste rock dump stability expertise. The use of test panels for evaluating cover alternatives is consistent with established mine closure practices and standard methodology in cover system design. However, specific documentation of this particular collaboration between GeoSystems and SRK for the Botadero Este cover system stability analysis was not independently verified from external sources.]

### Objetivo

El objetivo del presente estudio consiste en evaluar la estabilidad fisica de las coberturas segun las configuraciones propuestas bajo condiciones estaticas y dinamicas. El desempeno en condiciones reales de las coberturas en estos paneles de prueba sera evaluado para realizar los ajustes necesarios de cara al diseno definitivo de cierre.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The stated objective aligns with standard practice for mine closure cover system design. Evaluating physical stability under both static and dynamic conditions is required by industry guidelines, and the iterative approach of testing trial panels under field conditions before finalizing closure design is consistent with documented best practices in mine reclamation and risk-based closure methodology.]

### Alcances

Este es un estudio de gabinete de ingenieria a nivel basico, basado en la informacion suministrada por GeoSystems y Antamina. El analisis y validacion de esta informacion esta fuera del alcance del servicio de SRK.^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The description as a basic-level desktop engineering study based on client-provided information is consistent with standard consulting practice and typical phased approaches in geotechnical consulting. The explicit exclusion of data validation from scope is a standard limitation statement in engineering reports. However, this scope limitation means analysis quality depends critically on the accuracy of input data provided by others, which raises questions about verification rigor.]

El alcance del servicio se limita a proporcionar recomendaciones para la caracterizacion de los materiales de cobertura, revisar la amenaza sismica y realizar un analisis de desempeno de las alternativas de cobertura frente a escenarios estaticos y dinamicos. La estabilidad del botadero de base se analiza en forma aislada de manera de cuantificar la seguridad del sistema de soporte.^[Confidence: HIGH, Rationale: The defined scope elements are standard components of cover system stability analysis. Material characterization, seismic hazard review, and performance analysis under static and dynamic scenarios are fundamental requirements for geotechnical design of mine closure covers. Analyzing the underlying waste dump stability independently from the cover system is appropriate methodology to quantify foundation support capacity and is consistent with limit equilibrium and numerical modeling practices.]

El efecto de la erosion de la cobertura y el impacto de deformaciones diferidas en el botadero y su erosion quedan excluidos de este analisis.^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The explicit exclusion of cover erosion and delayed deformations represents a significant scope limitation. Industry guidelines recognize that erosion control and long-term movement are vital considerations in waste dump design and long-term cover performance. While this exclusion may be appropriate for an initial basic-level study, these factors would typically need to be addressed in detailed design phases for final closure. This limitation should be clearly understood when interpreting the study results.]
