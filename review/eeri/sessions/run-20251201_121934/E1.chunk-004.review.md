## Seismic Hazard

Geotechnical investigations show the site to be underlain by medium dense to very dense fill over glacial till. The glacial till is likely underlain by sandstone and siltstone but the borings do not show a consistent pattern throughout the site. The geophysical investigations show site $V_{s30}$ to range between 1,351 ft/s to 1,157 ft/s. The reservoir site is underlain by the Seattle Basin with the depth to basement rock over 7,000 meters. Ground motions considering basin effects and measured $V_{s30}$ were computed for eight earthquake cases including M9 and M7 on CSZ, M7 on SFZ and probabilistic ground motions for 72, 100, 475, 975, and 2,475-year return periods.
