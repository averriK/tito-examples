## Conclusions

The complex soil-structure interaction (SSI) response of buried reservoir cannot be accurately captured using traditional analysis. There are many factors that can only be modeled through an SSI analysis including nonlinear response of the soil, poorly compacted soil close to the reservoir wall, variation of passive soil along the wall height, radiation damping, and radial movement of roof and base slab. The SSI analysis for a range of ground motions provided a complete risk picture.
