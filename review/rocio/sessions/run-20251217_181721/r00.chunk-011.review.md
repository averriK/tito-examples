## 7. Filtraciones

### 7.1 Filtraciones 1

Aguas abajo de quebrada Titijones, en ladera derecha y contacto roca suelo morrénico, coordenadas de ubicación: E-334541, N-8125209, se ubica de media ladera superior, parte baja húmeda con desarrollo suelos saturados, el escurrimiento de agua esta originada por varios ojos de agua y precisamente en contacto de rocas andesíticas y depósitos morrénicos, la orientación e inclinación del basamento rocoso ayudan al desarrollo de las filtraciones; la fuente de agua lo constituye las altas cumbres del centro volcánico Iruma; no se ha medido el caudal, actualmente presenta bajo caudal, sin embargo durante épocas lluviosas se incrementa el flujo. (Apéndice B, Figura 55).

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: This paragraph describes groundwater seepage at the contact between andesitic rocks and morainic deposits in the Titijones ravine area. The hydrogeological mechanisms described are scientifically sound and consistent with established literature. Contact springs commonly occur at boundaries between permeable and impermeable rock layers, and seepage springs are characterized by oozing from numerous small openings in the soil [WEB:https://www.geological-digressions.com/springs-and-seeps/]. Andesitic volcanic aquifers exhibit complex hydrogeological features with perched aquifers in lava flows and pyroclastic complexes, supplying low discharge springs [WEB:https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10040-024-02784-0]. Morainic deposits can act as aquitards or conduits depending on their composition and structure [WEB:https://opentextbc.ca/geology/chapter/14-1-groundwater-and-aquifers/]. The description of seasonal flow variation is consistent with recharge-dependent spring behavior. However, confidence is rated MEDIUM due to: (1) the specific "centro volcánico Iruma" could not be verified through external sources - no published geological literature confirms the existence of a volcanic center with this name in Peru, though numerous other volcanic centers exist in southern Peru including Ticsani, Ubinas, Misti, and Huaynaputina [WEB:https://volcano.si.edu/volcanolist_countries.cfm?country=Peru]; (2) specific discharge measurements are explicitly stated as absent, limiting quantitative verification; (3) the precise geographic coordinates cannot be independently verified without field validation or detailed topographic/geological maps of the Titijones area.]

The seasonal variation in discharge (medium-low flow increasing during rainy seasons) reflects typical spring behavior dependent on precipitation recharge patterns. Confidence is rated MEDIUM for the same reasons as Filtración 1: (1) the "centro volcánico Iruma" remains unverified in external geological literature; (2) qualitative discharge descriptions (medio-bajo) lack quantitative measurements; (3) spatial coordinates and specific site characteristics cannot be independently confirmed without access to detailed regional geological maps or field surveys; (4) the referenced Apéndice B, Figura 56 is not available for verification in this audit. The hydrogeological conceptual model is sound, but the specific geographic and volumetric claims require field validation.]
