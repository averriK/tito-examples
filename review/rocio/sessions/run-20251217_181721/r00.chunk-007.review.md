## 3. Marco tectónico estructural

### 3.1 Falla tectónica local 1

Se presenta en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-335537, N-8124987, relacionado a la Fm Capillune, corresponde a una falla local con dirección N-335º-340º-E, longitud menor a 5 m, podría tener continuidad, lamentablemente está cubierta por depósitos morrénicos. (Apéndice B, Figura 48).

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The paragraph describes a local fault in road cut exposures along the Titijones-Suches road in the Puno region. The Formación Capillune is a well-documented Miocene-Pliocene lacustrine unit consisting of conglomerates, sandstones, clays, and redeposited tuffs, widely distributed across the Peruvian altiplano [WEB:https://infcis.iaea.org/udepo/Resources/Countries/Peru.pdf]. The Suches River region in Puno is a documented geological area with mining activities and geological studies [WEB:https://www.scielo.org.mx/scielo.php?pid=S2007-24222021000600145&script=sci_arttext_plus&tlng=en]. The azimuth notation N-335°-340°-E indicates strike direction measured clockwise from north, which is standard in geological mapping. UTM coordinates E-335537, N-8124987 are consistent with UTM Zone 18S or 19S used for southern Peru [WEB:https://epsg.io/32718]. The observation that moraine deposits obscure fault continuity is geologically plausible given the glacial history of the high Andes. However, confidence is medium rather than high because: (1) no external verification of this specific fault locality exists in published literature; (2) the azimuth notation "N-335º-340º-E" is somewhat unconventional (standard notation would be either a single value or a range without the "N" and "E" together); (3) the term "falla local" (local fault) lacks precise scale definition in formal geological terminology; and (4) the claimed fault length (<5 m) and limited exposure suggest minimal regional tectonic significance, making independent verification unlikely without accessing field data or referenced figures in Apéndice B.]

### 3.2 Falla tectónica local 2

Al igual que anterior falla local, se observa en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-335566, N-8125020, relacionado a la Fm Capillune, corresponde a una falla local tipo "graven", dirección N-335º-340º-E, longitud menor a 5 m, continuidad está cubierta por depósitos morrénicos. (Apéndice B, Figura 49).

^[Confidence: LOW, Rationale: This paragraph describes a second local fault approximately 30 meters north of the first (based on coordinate difference), also in Formación Capillune. The structural context is consistent with the previous description. However, confidence is low due to a critical terminological error: the text uses "graven" instead of the correct geological term "graben." A graben is a down-dropped block bounded by normal faults dipping toward each other, formed by extensional tectonics [WEB:https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graben] [WEB:https://www.britannica.com/science/graben]. The misspelling raises questions about technical rigor and whether the author correctly identified the fault geometry. A proper graben structure requires bilateral normal faulting, which cannot be adequately characterized in a <5 m exposure length. The coordinates (E-335566, N-8125020) place this feature only 33 m from the first fault, and both share identical strike orientations (N-335°-340°-E), which could suggest they are segments of the same structure rather than independent faults. The description lacks sufficient structural detail (dip angles, displacement magnitude, sense of motion) to confirm graben geometry. The notation inconsistencies and terminology error significantly reduce confidence in the structural interpretation, though the general geological context (Fm Capillune, moraine cover, road cut exposure) remains plausible.]

### 3.3 Falla tectónica local 3

Se observa en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-333240, N-8123224, Se desarrolla en tobas de la Fm. Sencca, es una falla local que cruza la carretera, con dirección N-330º-340º-E, longitud menor a 6 m, trayectoria hacia la parte superior está cubierta por suelos residuales. (Apéndice B, Figura 50).

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: This paragraph describes a third local fault approximately 2.3 km west-southwest of faults 1-2, developed in tuffs of the Formación Sencca rather than Formación Capillune. The Formación Sencca is a documented ignimbritic (welded tuff) formation in southern Peru with deposits 300-500 m thick deposited during the Tertiary period [WEB:https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huaynaputina]. The Sencca Formation is grouped with Capillune as older Tertiary rock units in the region, and rhyolitic tuff flows have been extensively documented in southern Peru [WEB:https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/626331]. The change in host lithology from lacustrine sediments (Capillune) to volcanic tuffs (Sencca) is geologically significant and affects fault expression. The strike orientation N-330°-340°-E shows a slight clockwise rotation compared to faults 1-2, consistent with local structural variation. The observation that residual soils obscure the upper trajectory is reasonable for weathered volcanic terrains in the high Andes. Confidence is medium because: (1) the description provides better geological context (crosscutting road, specific lithology); (2) fault length is slightly better constrained (<6 m); but (3) no external sources verify this specific locality; (4) the azimuth range notation remains unconventional; and (5) structural details (dip, displacement, kinematics) are absent, preventing full assessment of the fault's tectonic significance.]

### 3.4 Falla tectónica local 4

Se observa en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-333230, N-8123159, Se presenta en tobas de la Fm. Sencca, corresponde a una falla local que cruza la carretera, con dirección N-330º-340º-E, longitud menor a 6 m, trayectoria hacia la parte superior está cubierta por suelos residuales. (Apéndice B, Figura 51).

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: This paragraph describes a fourth local fault located only 10 m west and 65 m south of fault 3, with nearly identical characteristics: same host rock (Fm. Sencca tuffs), same strike orientation (N-330°-340°-E), same length constraint (<6 m), same upper trajectory obscured by residual soils, and same road-cutting relationship. The extreme proximity and identical parameters strongly suggest these may be segments of the same fault structure or a closely-spaced fault zone rather than truly independent faults. The Formación Sencca volcanic tuff context remains well-supported [WEB:https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huaynaputina]. However, the near-duplication of descriptions raises methodological concerns: (1) the distinction between faults 3 and 4 may be artificial if they represent one continuous or en-echelon structure; (2) independent documentation in separate appendix figures (50 vs. 51) for features 75 m apart suggests possibly excessive subdivision; (3) the consistent 10-degree azimuth uncertainty range (N-330°-340°-E) for all described faults hints at measurement limitations rather than true structural variation; (4) the repeated phrase "trayectoria hacia la parte superior está cubierta por suelos residuales" indicates systematic exposure limitations that hamper structural interpretation. Confidence remains medium due to valid geological context but questionable classification as distinct fault entities.]
