## 3. Marco tectonico estructural

### 3.1 Falla tectonica local 1

Se presenta en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-335537, N-8124987, relacionado a la Fm Capillune, corresponde a una falla local con direccion N-335º-340º-E, longitud menor a 5 m, podria tener continuidad, lamentablemente esta cubierta por depositos morrenicos. (Apendice B, Figura 48).
^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: This describes a small-scale local fault in Capillune Formation observed in road cuts. The Central Andes of southern Peru are tectonically active with numerous local faults [@WEB:http://www.andeangeology.cl]. The NW-SE orientation is consistent with regional structural trends. The specific coordinates, orientation, and length represent primary field observations that cannot be independently verified. The observation that moraine deposits obscure fault continuation is geologically plausible given the glacial history of the high Andes. All three sessions rated MEDIUM confidence, noting valid geological context but inability to externally verify specific field observations.]

### 3.2 Falla tectonica local 2

Al igual que anterior falla local, se observa en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-335566, N-8125020, relacionado a la Fm Capillune, corresponde a una falla local tipo "graven", direccion N-335º-340º-E, longitud menor a 5 m, continuidad esta cubierta por depositos morrenicos. (Apendice B, Figura 49).
^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Field observation of a small graben-type local fault in Capillune Formation. The graben structure (normal faulting) is consistent with extensional tectonics common in volcanic plateau regions. Coverage by morainic deposits is geologically reasonable. Sessions noted the term "graven" should be "graben" (a down-dropped block bounded by normal faults formed by extension), but the structural interpretation remains plausible. The proximity to fault 1 (approximately 33-35 m apart based on coordinates) with similar orientation suggests they may be part of the same fault system. Two sessions rated MEDIUM confidence; one rated LOW due to the terminology error, but consensus is MEDIUM given the valid geological context.]

### 3.3 Falla tectonica local 3

Se observa en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-333240, N-8123224, Se desarrolla en tobas de la Fm. Sencca, es una falla local que cruza la carretera, con direccion N-330º-340º-E, longitud menor a 6 m, trayectoria hacia la parte superior esta cubierta por suelos residuales. (Apendice B, Figura 50).
^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Local fault in Formacion Sencca dacitic tuffs crossing the road. The Formacion Sencca is well-documented as ignimbritic deposits in southern Peru [@WEB:https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huaynaputina]. Faults affecting Miocene-Pliocene tuffs are geologically plausible given post-depositional tectonic deformation. The orientation (N-330º-340º-E, NW-SE) is consistent with regional structural patterns. Coverage by residual soils is expected in weathered volcanic terrain. This fault is located approximately 2.3 km southwest of faults 1-2. All three sessions rated MEDIUM confidence, noting valid geological context but inability to verify specific field observations.]

### 3.4 Falla tectonica local 4

Se observa en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-333230, N-8123159, Se presenta en tobas de la Fm. Sencca, corresponde a una falla local que cruza la carretera, con direccion N-330º-340º-E, longitud menor a 6 m, trayectoria hacia la parte superior esta cubierta por suelos residuales. (Apendice B, Figura 51).
^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Another local fault in Sencca Formation tuffs with similar characteristics to fault 3. Located only 65-75 m from fault 3 (based on coordinates) with matching orientation (N-330º-340º-E) and similar exposed length. The close spacing and parallel orientation suggest these may be part of a fault zone or conjugate fault system. The consistent NW-SE orientation across all four faults indicates regional structural control affecting the Titijones area. All three sessions rated MEDIUM confidence, with sessions noting that the proximity and identical characteristics suggest these may be segments of the same structure rather than independent faults.]
