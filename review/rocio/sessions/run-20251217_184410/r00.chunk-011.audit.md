## 7. Filtraciones

### 7.1 Filtraciones 1

Aguas abajo de quebrada Titijones, en ladera derecha y contacto roca suelo morrenico, coordenadas de ubicacion: E-334541, N-8125209, se ubica de media ladera superior, parte baja humeda con desarrollo suelos saturados, el escurrimiento de agua esta originada por varios ojos de agua y precisamente en contacto de rocas andesiticas y depositos morrenicos, la orientacion e inclinacion del basamento rocoso ayudan al desarrollo de las filtraciones; la fuente de agua lo constituye las altas cumbres del centro volcanico Iruma; no se ha medido el caudal, actualmente presenta bajo caudal, sin embargo durante epocas lluviosas se incrementa el flujo. (Apendice B, Figura 55).
^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: The description of seeps at the rock-soil contact between andesitic bedrock and morainic deposits is hydrogeologically sound and typical of Andean volcanic terrains. This represents a classic "perched aquifer" or "contact spring" scenario where permeable morainic deposits overlie less permeable volcanic bedrock, and groundwater flows along the contact interface until it emerges as seepage. Contact springs commonly occur at boundaries between permeable and impermeable rock layers [@WEB:https://www.geological-digressions.com]. Andesitic volcanic aquifers exhibit complex hydrogeological features with perched aquifers in lava flows and pyroclastic complexes, supplying low discharge springs [@DOI:10.1007/s10040-024-02784-0]. The report correctly identifies that "the orientation and inclination of the rocky basement help develop the seeps," which is a fundamental hydrogeological principle: dipping impermeable layers channel groundwater flow toward discharge points. Morainic deposits have "high porosity and permeability" [@WEB:https://www.sciencedirect.com], and their contact with volcanic bedrock commonly produces seepage zones. The seasonal variability is entirely consistent with shallow groundwater systems recharged by precipitation. Sessions rated MEDIUM to HIGH confidence, with consensus recognizing sound hydrogeological mechanisms but noting "centro volcanico Iruma" could not be verified in published literature and discharge measurements are absent.]

### 7.2 Filtracion 2

Aguas abajo de quebrada Titijones, en ladera derecha y contacto roca suelo morrenico, coordenadas referencial: E-333809, N-8124777, se ubica varios ojos de agua desarrollados en media ladera superior, los flujos discurren hacia la margen derecha de la quebrada Titijones; la disposicion del basamento rocoso en direccion hacia la quebrada Titijones, ayudan al flujo que nacen en las partes altas del centro volcanico Iruma; actualmente con caudal medio-bajo, se incrementa durante epocas lluviosas. (Apendice B, Figura 56).
^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: This second seep location exhibits the same hydrogeological pattern as Filtraciones 1, reinforcing the conceptual model of contact-controlled groundwater discharge. The spatial clustering of multiple seepage points along the same stratigraphic contact is characteristic of linear spring systems controlled by lithologic boundaries. The coordinates indicate this seep is located ~800m southwest of Filtraciones 1, both on the right margin of quebrada Titijones, suggesting a continuous hydrogeological feature along this contact zone. The description that "the arrangement of the rocky basement in the direction toward quebrada Titijones helps the flow" correctly identifies structural control of groundwater flow paths. Research confirms that groundwater in volcanic mountain terrain of southern Peru follows "flow paths that convey groundwater to lower elevations where the springs emerge" [@DOI:10.5194/hess-26-483-2022]. The reported seasonal variability matches Filtraciones 1, supporting shared hydrogeological control mechanism. Sessions rated MEDIUM to HIGH confidence, with same rationale as Filtraciones 1 regarding valid hydrogeological framework but unverifiable specific volcanic center nomenclature and qualitative discharge descriptions.]
