## 8. Estaciones de Bombeo

### 8.1 Estacion de Bombeo (TW-2A)

Corresponde a una estacion de Bombeo "operativo", se ubica en la amplia llanura fluvioglaciar de pampa Titijones, sobre una superficie llana, delimitada por cerca, coordenada de ubicacion: E-338579, N-8126363, cota 4503, profundidad referencial 300m. (Apendice B, Figura 57).
^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: The description of an operational pumping station on the fluvioglacial plain with a reference depth of 300m is consistent with documented groundwater extraction infrastructure in the Titijones aquifer system. Southern Peru Copper Corporation has "pumped ground water from the Titijones area for use at Cuajone for many years" [@DOI:10.3390/w16050769], with water rights for "up to 1,950 liters per second from well fields at the Huaitire, Vizcachas and Titijones aquifers" [@WEB:https://southerncoppercorp.com]. The 300m depth is appropriate for accessing the Formacion Capillune aquifer, which the report describes as extending to significant depths (the C-5 borehole reached 568m). The location on a flat fluvioglacial plain is typical for production well siting, where thick permeable sediments and minimal topographic relief facilitate well installation. The elevation of 4503 masl is consistent with the high-altitude setting of the Titijones aquifer system above 4000 masl in the Andean Altiplano. Sessions rated MEDIUM to HIGH confidence, recognizing consistency with documented SPCC operations but noting specific well designation and details cannot be independently verified.]

### 8.2 Estacion de Bombeo (TW-03)

La estacion de bombeo (TW-03) actualmente "operativo", se ubica en la amplia llanura fluvioglaciar de pampa Titijones, sobre una superficie llana, delimitada por cerca, coordenada de ubicacion: E-338976, N-8125656, cota 4528, profundidad referencial 300m. Apendice B, Figura 58).
^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: This second pumping station exhibits identical characteristics to TW-2A: operational status, location on the fluvioglacial plain, 300m reference depth, and similar elevation (4528 masl vs. 4503 masl). The spatial separation of ~780m from TW-2A is typical of wellfield design to minimize interference between pumping wells while maximizing aquifer capture. The slightly higher elevation (25m difference) suggests TW-03 is positioned further up-gradient in the aquifer system. Multiple pumping stations are consistent with the scale of water extraction documented for SPCC operations. Historical data indicate that "SPCC has pumped ground water from the Titijones area for use at Cuajone for many years, depleting most of the local surface water" [@WEB:https://remwater.org]. The fenced enclosures indicate security and operational control, standard for industrial water supply facilities. Sessions rated MEDIUM to HIGH confidence, with one session noting minor formatting error (missing opening parenthesis).]

### 8.3 Estacion de Bombeo (TW-04)

Comprende a estacion de Bombeo actualmente "operativo", sobre amplia llanura fluvioglaciar de pampa Titijones, protegida con cerca de seguridad, coordenada de ubicacion: E-339560, N-8124863, cota 4570, profundidad referencial 300m. (Apendice B, Figura 59).
^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: The third pumping station maintains the same operational and technical characteristics as TW-2A and TW-03. The elevation of 4570 masl (67m higher than TW-2A, 42m higher than TW-03) indicates this well is positioned further up-gradient, potentially capturing recharge closer to its source areas. The spatial distribution of the three wells across pampa Titijones suggests strategic wellfield layout designed to maximize aquifer interception. The consistent 300m depth across all three stations indicates targeting of the same productive horizons within the Formacion Capillune. Water infrastructure for the Cuajone mine "begins with a water supply at Lake Suche at 14,500 feet in the Andes" [@WEB:https://www.xylem.com], with the Titijones wellfield representing a key component of this system. The operational status of all three stations confirms active extraction from the aquifer system at the time of the report. Sessions rated MEDIUM to HIGH confidence, with consensus recognizing factual documentation consistent with independently confirmed SPCC operations but noting lack of pumping rates, water levels, and well construction details.]
