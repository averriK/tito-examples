## 4. Marco Neotectonico

Corresponde a una traza de menor de 3 km longitud, se observa en la cresta de una morrena, tiene orientacion NO-SE; no se observa la continuidad en la parte baja, posiblemente borrada y/o cubierta por depositos fluvioglaciares. (Figura 4-1)
^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The description of a <3 km neotectonic fault trace on a moraine crest with NW-SE orientation is consistent with neotectonic activity in southern Peru. INGEMMET has identified 24 active geological faults in Moquegua department with NW-SE direction that have reactivated in the last 10,000 years [@WEB:https://www.gob.pe/institucion/ingemmet]. The observation that lower continuation is obscured by fluvioglacial deposits is geomorphologically reasonable for glaciated regions. All three sessions rated MEDIUM confidence, noting the description is regionally plausible but specific coordinates and fault name are lacking for external verification.]

### 4.2 Falla Neotectonica Ticsani - Suches

Se ubica en el lado septentrional del area de estudio, longitud aproximada 8 Km, direccion N-340º-350º, subvertical, tipo normal. Presenta trayectoria sinuosa a irregular a discontinua, los segmentos de falla muestran escarpes con morfologia bien conservada, cuyas alturas alcanzan hasta 2.00 m cortando flujos lavicos del centro volcanico Titine y Arichaua y depositos morrenicos y aluviales del Holoceno. (Figura 4-2).
^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: The Ticsani-Suches neotectonic fault system is regionally consistent. Ticsani volcano has been associated with seismic activity, including a seismic crisis in October 2005 related to local faulting [@WEB:https://repositorio.igp.gob.pe]. The N-340º-350º orientation (NNW-SSE) is consistent with NW-SE trending active faults documented in Moquegua [@WEB:https://www.gob.pe/institucion/ingemmet]. Normal fault scarps up to 2 m high cutting Holocene volcanic flows and moraines are typical indicators of active faults. However, the specific volcanic centers "Titine" and "Arichaua" could not be verified in published literature or volcanic databases. Sessions rated MEDIUM to HIGH confidence, with consensus recognizing the regional neotectonic framework is sound but specific volcanic nomenclature lacks external verification.]
