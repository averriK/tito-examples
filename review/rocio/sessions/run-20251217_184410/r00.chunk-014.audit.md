## 10. Cursos de agua

### 10.1 Curso de agua 1

En la carretera Titijones en direccion hacia Suches y proximo a vivienda de comunero, se presenta dos manantiales los cuales discurren sus aguas hacia la quebrada Titijones, coordenadas de ubicacion: E-340860 y N-8124765; E-340674, N-8124823; inmediatamente agua abajo y ambas margenes se desarrolla bofedales y humedales sobre extensa llanura fluvioglaciar, las aguas discurren en direccion NO-SE.^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: All three sessions show strong consensus on the hydrogeological sequence described. Session 1 rated MEDIUM confidence noting the description is consistent with Section 5 springs and that wetland development downstream is hydrologically consistent. Session 2 also rated MEDIUM confidence, confirming bofedales on fluvioglacial plains are well-documented in the southern Peruvian Andes and that Titijones is a documented aquifer system, though specific coordinates cannot be verified. Session 3 rated HIGH confidence, emphasizing the internal consistency with Section 5, the well-documented groundwater-surface water interactions, and the characteristic pattern of wetland development on fluvioglacial plains. The consensus reflects high confidence in the hydrogeological characterization with moderate uncertainty regarding specific coordinate verification.]

Origen de los manantiales posiblemente por control litologico-estructural del basamento volcanico, las aguas son provenientes de las altas cumbres del cerro Limani y Tutupaca; principal fuente se estima caudal de 20 l/s, mientras el segundo manantial se estima en 0.5 l/s. (Apendice B, Figura 46_52)^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: The three sessions reached consensus that the lithologic-structural control mechanism is geologically sound but lacks independent verification. Session 1 rated MEDIUM, noting this reiterates interpretation from Section 5. Session 2 also rated MEDIUM, confirming Tutupaca volcano exists with documented hot springs and structural controls in southern Peru, though "Cerro Limani" could not be verified and discharge estimates are within reasonable ranges but cannot be independently verified. Session 3 rated MEDIUM, repeating the same confidence assessment as Section 5. All sessions agree the conceptual model is hydrogeologically plausible but requires quantitative verification of recharge attribution and flow measurements.]

### 10.2 Curso de agua 2

Corresponde al curso superior de la quebrada Titijones, cuyas aguas se originan mayormente en dos manantiales ubicadas proximo a vivienda de comunero; coordenadas de ubicacion: E-340860 y N-8124765; E-340674, N-8124823; aguas abajo discurre de manera meandriforme a sinuosa, baja pendiente, con aportes de tributarios en algunos casos delimitados por bofedales y humedales; cauce principal corta a la amplia llanura fluvioglaciar, en general tiene una orientacion de angulo recto invertido, posiblemente causado por control tectonico-estructural del basamento volcanico. (Apendice B, Figura 61)^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: The three sessions show consensus on the fluvial geomorphology description but acknowledge the structural control interpretation requires verification. Session 1 rated MEDIUM confidence noting the meandering pattern on low-gradient plains is consistent with established geomorphology, and structural control of drainage patterns is plausible but cannot be independently verified. Session 2 also rated MEDIUM, confirming meandering patterns and right-angle drainage are well-documented in structurally controlled terrains, though the specific "inverted right angle" orientation cannot be verified. Session 3 rated MEDIUM-HIGH, adding that the neotectonic faults documented in Section 4.2 support the structural control hypothesis, though definitive confirmation requires lineament analysis. The consensus reflects confidence in the geomorphological characterization with moderate uncertainty regarding specific structural control claims.]

### 10.3 Curso de agua 3

Corresponde al curso medio de la quebrada Titijones ubicada en el lado meridional de la cuenca Titijones, aguas abajo discurre de manera meandriforme a sinuosa, baja pendiente, con aportes de tributarios en algunos casos delimitados por bofedales y humedales; cauce principal sigue un valle ligeramente cerrado cuya orientacion NE-SO posiblemente originado por control tectonico-estructural del basamento volcanico. (Apendice B, Figura 62_63)^[Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, Rationale: All three sessions reached similar conclusions for the middle reach description. Session 1 rated MEDIUM confidence, noting the morphology is consistent with fluvial geomorphology principles and structural control is plausible, though specific interpretation cannot be independently verified. Session 2 also rated MEDIUM, confirming the geomorphological description aligns with principles and that NE-SW orientation could reflect fault control, though specific verification is lacking. Session 3 rated MEDIUM-HIGH, noting the NE-SW orientation is consistent with documented fault trends (N-330 to 340-E) and the transition from open plain to slightly closed valley is typical downstream evolution. All sessions agree the characterization is geomorphologically sound but structural control attribution requires analytical verification through lineament or structural analysis.]
