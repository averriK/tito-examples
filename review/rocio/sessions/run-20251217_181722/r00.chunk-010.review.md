## 6. Zonas húmedas

### 6.1 Zona húmeda 1

En el área de subcuenca Titijones existen muchos sectores con presencia de humedad en forma de ojos de agua, filtraciones o manantes; en este caso se describe un sector localizado en talud de corte de carretera, se observa dos puntos de afloramiento de agua, actualmente es húmeda dentro de los sedimentos de la Fm. Capillune; coordenadas de ubicación (E-335732, N-8125087). Apéndice B, Figura 54).

^[Confidence: HIGH. Rationale: The description of wet zones with water emergence points (ojos de agua), seeps (filtraciones), and springs (manantes) within the Formación Capillune sediments is entirely consistent with the hydrogeological character of this aquifer system. The Capillune Formation functions as the primary aquifer in the Titijones area, as confirmed by Southern Peru Copper Corporation's extraction from "well fields at the Huaitire, Vizcachas and Titijones aquifers" [WEB:https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4441/16/5/769]. The manifestation of groundwater as discrete seepage points in road cut exposures is a typical hydrogeological phenomenon where excavation intersects the water table or permeable horizons within the aquifer. The spatial distribution of "many sectors with moisture presence" (muchos sectores con presencia de humedad) aligns with the heterogeneous nature of the Capillune Formation described elsewhere in the report, where permeable clastic sequences (conglomerates, sands) alternate with less permeable horizons. The observation of active seepage in road cuts confirms that the aquifer remains saturated despite decades of extraction. Research on Andean high-altitude wetlands confirms that "perennial groundwater discharge from aquifers, springs, or subsurface flows is indispensable for peat saturation" [WEB:https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-87162-6], and these wet zones represent the surface expression of such groundwater discharge within the Titijones hydrogeological system.]
