## 3. Marco tectónico estructural

### 3.1 Falla tectónica local 1

Se presenta en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-335537, N-8124987, relacionado a la Fm Capillune, corresponde a una falla local con dirección N-335º-340º-E, longitud menor a 5 m, podría tener continuidad, lamentablemente está cubierta por depósitos morrénicos. (Apéndice B, Figura 48).

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: This describes a small-scale local fault in the Capillune Formation observed in a road cut. The specific coordinates, orientation (N-335º-340º-E), and length (<5 m) represent primary field observations that cannot be independently verified. The observation that continuation is obscured by morainic deposits is geologically plausible. The Central Andes of southern Peru are tectonically active with numerous local faults [WEB:http://www.andeangeology.cl/index.php/revista1/article/view/V47n2-3168/html_1]. The NW-SE orientation is consistent with regional structural trends.]

### 3.2 Falla tectónica local 2

Al igual que anterior falla local, se observa en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-335566, N-8125020, relacionado a la Fm Capillune, corresponde a una falla local tipo "graven", dirección N-335º-340º-E, longitud menor a 5 m, continuidad está cubierta por depósitos morrénicos. (Apéndice B, Figura 49).

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Field observation of a small graben-type local fault in Capillune Formation. The coordinates, orientation matching fault 1, and <5 m length are primary field data. The graben structure (normal faulting) is consistent with extensional tectonics common in volcanic plateau regions. Like the previous fault, coverage by morainic deposits limiting observation is geologically reasonable. The proximity and similar orientation to fault 1 (approximately 35 m apart) suggests they may be part of the same fault system.]

### 3.3 Falla tectónica local 3

Se observa en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-333240, N-8123224, Se desarrolla en tobas de la Fm. Sencca, es una falla local que cruza la carretera, con dirección N-330º-340º-E, longitud menor a 6 m, trayectoria hacia la parte superior está cubierta por suelos residuales. (Apéndice B, Figura 50).

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Local fault in Sencca Formation dacitic tuffs crossing the road. The coordinates, orientation (N-330º-340º-E), and <6 m exposed length are field observations. The similar strike to faults 1 and 2 (NW-SE) suggests a consistent local stress field. Coverage by residual soils upslope is expected in weathered volcanic terrain. This fault is located approximately 2.3 km southwest of faults 1-2, indicating distributed small-scale faulting in the area.]

### 3.4 Falla tectónica local 4

Se observa en talud de corte de carretera Titijones a Suches, coordenada referencial: E-333230, N-8123159, Se presenta en tobas de la Fm. Sencca, corresponde a una falla local que cruza la carretera, con dirección N-330º-340º-E, longitud menor a 6 m, trayectoria hacia la parte superior está cubierta por suelos residuales. (Apéndice B, Figura 51).

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Another local fault in Sencca Formation tuffs with similar characteristics to fault 3. Located only 65 m from fault 3 (based on coordinates) with matching orientation (N-330º-340º-E) and similar exposed length (<6 m). The close spacing and parallel orientation suggest these may be part of a fault zone or conjugate fault system. The consistent NW-SE orientation across all four faults indicates a regional structural control affecting the Titijones area.]
