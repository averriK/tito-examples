### 4.2 Falla Neotectónica Ticsani – Suches

Se ubica en el lado septentrional del área de estudio, longitud aproximada 8 Km, dirección N-340º-350º, subvertical, tipo normal. Presenta trayectoria sinuosa a irregular a discontinua, los segmentos de falla muestran escarpes con morfología bien conservada, cuyas alturas alcanzan hasta 2.00 m cortando flujos lávicos del centro volcánico Titine y Arichaua y depósitos morrénicos y aluviales del Holoceno. (Figura 4-2).

^[Confidence: MEDIUM, Rationale: Description of the Ticsani-Suches neotectonic fault system in the northern study area. The characteristics described (approximately 8 km length, N-340º-350º orientation [NNW-SSE], subvertical, normal fault type) are consistent with neotectonic faulting patterns in southern Peru where active faults show NW-SE directions [WEB:https://www.gob.pe/institucion/ingemmet/noticias/345818-moquegua-ingemmet-identifica-24-fallas-geologicas-activas-que-se-reactivaron-en-los-ultimos-10-mil-anos]. Fault scarps up to 2 m high cutting Holocene volcanic flows and morainic/alluvial deposits indicate recent activity within the last 10,000 years, matching criteria for active faults in Moquegua. The sinuous to irregular discontinuous trace is typical of normal faulting in volcanic terrain. However, this specific fault cannot be independently verified.]

Cutting of Holocene volcanic flows from Titine and Limani centers and morainic deposits confirms recent activity. The consistent orientation, geometry, and kinematics between northern and southern segments indicate a coherent fault system. Well-preserved scarp morphology is consistent with youthful fault movement typical of active faults in the region.]
