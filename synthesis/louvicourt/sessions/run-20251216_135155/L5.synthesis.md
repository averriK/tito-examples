## SLOT 1: Seismotectonic Setting Summary

The Louvicourt site is located within a stable continental region tectonic environment characteristic of the Canadian Shield, where seismicity arises from intraplate deformation rather than active plate boundary processes. The region exhibits widely distributed seismicity not associated with mapped surface fault traces, with lower earthquake occurrence rates and diffuse epicenter distributions typical of stable continental interior zones. The seismic source model adopted for the Louvicourt site uses the CanadaSHM6 model developed by the Geological Survey of Canada without modification, with all seismic sources classified as Stable Continental Region sources. Within the 1,000 km region surrounding the site, no sources are classified as active crustal tectonic types associated with plate boundaries or subduction zones.
