### SLOT 3: Seismic Record Selection Summary

The seismic record selection methodology for the Louvicourt TSF site developed two suites of eight two-horizontal-component earthquake acceleration time histories amplitude-scaled to match the mean uniform hazard spectrum for 1/10,000 annual exceedance probability. The methodology addressed two distinct fundamental periods: a shorter period range from 0.25 s to 0.37 s (with conditioning period of 0.31 s and period range of interest from 0.062 s to 0.62 s), and a longer fundamental period of approximately 1.15 s (with period range of interest from 0.23 s to 2.3 s). The causal parameters for record selection were derived from disaggregation analysis results at 1/10,000 AEP, establishing a target scenario with moment magnitude 7.3, rupture distance 170 km, hypocentral depth 20 km, depth to top of rupture 10 km, and rupture plane dip 90 degrees. However, the actual selection criteria for the candidate ground motion catalog explicitly relaxed these constraints, permitting magnitudes from $M_w$ 6.0 to $M_w$ 7.8 and distances from 0 km to 200 km. The selected earthquake acceleration time histories exhibited limitations in their match to the stated causal parameter criteria, particularly regarding source-to-site distances and earthquake magnitudes.
